# Nova Bank NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS Charges Policy

This document outlines the policies regarding NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS transactions at Nova Bank. It includes details on eligibility, fees, limits, timelines, conditions, application processes, escalation procedures, and FAQs.

## Eligibility

1. **Account Holders**: Only registered Nova Bank account holders are eligible to use NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS services. Account types include Savings, Current, and Corporate accounts.
2. **Minimum Balance**: A minimum average monthly balance of INR 5,000 is required for Savings accounts to access these services without additional charges.
3. **Registration**: Customers must register for digital banking services, either via the Nova Bank app or internet banking portal, to initiate NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS transactions.

## Fees and Charges

1. **NEFT Charges**: 
   - Transactions up to INR 10,000: INR 2.50 per transaction.
   - Transactions between INR 10,001 and INR 1,00,000: INR 5.00 per transaction.
   - Transactions above INR 1,00,000: INR 15.00 per transaction.

2. **RTGS Charges**:
   - Transactions between INR 2,00,000 and INR 5,00,000: INR 25.00 per transaction.
   - Transactions above INR 5,00,000: INR 50.00 per transaction.

3. **IMPS Charges**:
   - Transactions up to INR 1,00,000: INR 5.00 per transaction.
   - Transactions above INR 1,00,000: INR 8.00 per transaction.
   
## Limits

1. **NEFT**: Maximum transaction limit is INR 10,00,000 per day per account.
2. **RTGS**: Minimum transaction limit is INR 2,00,000 with a maximum of INR 25,00,000 per transaction.
3. **IMPS**: Maximum transaction limit is INR 2,00,000 per day per account.

## Timelines

1. **NEFT**: Available from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekdays and 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays. Transactions initiated beyond these hours will be processed the next working day.
2. **RTGS**: Operates from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 1:30 PM on Saturdays. Transactions outside these hours will be queued for the next working day.
3. **IMPS**: Available 24/7, including Sundays and bank holidays, for immediate fund transfers.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Sufficient Funds**: Transactions will not be processed if the account does not have sufficient funds, inclusive of applicable charges.
2. **Technical Failures**: Nova Bank is not responsible for delays due to technical failures beyond its control, including connectivity issues.
3. **Refunds for Failed Transfers**: Charges for failed transactions due to bank errors will be refunded within 7 working days.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **NEFT/RTGS/IMPS Registration**: Register through the Nova Bank app or internet banking by navigating to the 'Fund Transfer' section and selecting 'Register for NEFT/RTGS/IMPS'.
2. **Claiming Refunds**: Customers must submit a refund request form available on the Nova Bank website or mobile app. The form requires transaction ID, date, and a brief description of the issue.
3. **Processing Time**: Refund requests are processed within 10 working days from the date of submission.

## Escalation

1. **First Level**: Contact Nova Bank's customer service at 1800-123-4567 or email support@novabank.com. Expect a response within 3 working days.
2. **Second Level**: If unresolved, escalate to the Branch Manager of your home branch. A written complaint can be submitted in person.
3. **Final Level**: For unresolved issues beyond 10 working days, approach the Banking Ombudsman. Further details are available on the Nova Bank website.

## FAQs

1. **What are the cutoff times for NEFT and RTGS transactions?**
   - NEFT: 7:00 PM on weekdays, 1:00 PM on Saturdays.
   - RTGS: 4:30 PM on weekdays, 1:30 PM on Saturdays.

2. **Are there any charges for IMPS transactions on Sundays?**
   - Yes, standard charges apply for IMPS transactions at all times, including Sundays and holidays.

3. **How soon can I expect my refund for a failed transaction?**
   - Refunds are typically processed within 7 working days for bank-induced transaction failures.

This policy is subject to periodic review and may be updated to reflect changes in regulatory requirements or operational procedures.
