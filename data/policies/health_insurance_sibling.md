# Nova Shield Family Floater Health Plan Terms

## 1. Plan Coverage Tiers and Sum Insured Matrix
Nova Shield Insurance Company offers a family-centric health insurance option under the **Family Floater Health Plan** with three sum insured choices:
* **Base Sum Insured Tiers**: Option A: INR 4,00,000; Option B: INR 8,00,000; Option C: INR 20,00,000. Available for individual and family floater coverage for the primary policyholder, legally married spouse, and up to 2 dependent unmarried children aged 91 days to 21 years.
* **Entry Age**: Adults aged 18 years to 60 years with lifetime renewability. Children are eligible from day 91 under family floater terms.

## 2. Hospital Room Rent and Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Sub-Limits
Hospital accommodation costs are controlled by specific percentage caps based on the selected base sum insured:
* **Standard Room Accommodation**: Limited to 0.75% of the base sum insured per day of hospitalization (e.g., INR 3,000 per day for an INR 4,00,000 policy; INR 6,000 per day for an INR 8,00,000 policy).
* **Intensive Care Unit (ICU) / ICCU Charges**: Capped at 1.75% of the base sum insured daily (INR 7,000 per day for a 4-lakh sum insured; INR 14,000 per day for an 8-lakh sum insured).
* **Proportionate Tariff Deduction Clause**: If the insured opts for a room category exceeding the daily ceiling, associated medical expenses (physician fees, nursing charges, operating room charges, diagnostic tests) are proportionately decreased by the ratio of (eligible room rent / actual room rent). This deduction excludes costs for medicines, consumables, and implants.

## 3. Mandatory Waiting Periods and Specific Disease Restrictions
* **Initial Waiting Period**: 45 calendar days from policy start. No claims for treatment or hospitalization will be accepted during this period, except for emergencies due to accidental injury.
* **Pre-Existing Diseases (PED)**: A waiting period of 30 months (2.5 years) of continuous policy tenure is required before claims related to pre-existing conditions (e.g., hypertension, diabetes, asthma) become eligible for reimbursement.
* **Specific Named Ailments (18-Month Waiting Period)**: A waiting period of 18 months applies before coverage is available for: cataract, benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia, hydrocele, anal fistula, piles, stones in urinary or biliary systems, hysterectomy for non-cancerous conditions, and joint replacement surgery (except if necessitated by an accident).
* **Cataract Specific Sub-Limit**: Cataract surgery expenses are capped at a maximum of INR 25,000 per eye, or actual costs, whichever is lower.

## 4. Inpatient, Pre/Post-Hospitalization, and Day Care Benefits
* **Inpatient Hospitalization**: Covers boarding, nursing, surgeon fees, blood, oxygen, and prosthetic implants provided the hospitalization exceeds 24 hours continuously.
* **Pre-Hospitalization Coverage**: Includes diagnostics, consultations, and medications up to 45 days before hospital admission.
* **Post-Hospitalization Coverage**: Covers follow-up medical costs, pathology tests, and physiotherapy up to 75 days post discharge.
* **Day-Care Medical Procedures**: Includes 460 specified interventions and therapies (e.g., chemotherapy, radiotherapy, dialysis, eye surgeries) requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalization due to advanced technology.
* **Emergency Road Ambulance**: Reimburses up to INR 1,800 per hospitalization event for transport to the nearest equipped medical facility.
* **Organ Donor Hospitalization**: Expenses for organ harvesting surgery on the donor are covered up to INR 1,50,000 or 8% of the sum insured, whichever is lower.

## 5. Cumulative No-Claim Bonus (NCB) Structure
* Policyholders who complete a policy year without claims receive a 15% increase in base sum insured as a Cumulative No-Claim Bonus, with no premium hike.
* The Cumulative Bonus can accumulate annually up to a maximum of 60% of the original sum insured (e.g., maximum INR 2,40,000 bonus on a 4-lakh policy).
* If a claim is made during any policy year, the accumulated NCB decreases by 15% at renewal, but the base sum insured will not fall below the initial policy limit.

## 6. Claim Intimation Timelines and Cashless Settlement Process
* **Planned Hospitalization**: Notify Nova Shield Third Party Administrator (TPA) at least 72 hours before hospital admission.
* **Emergency Hospitalization**: Notify within 12 hours of admission or before discharge, whichever is sooner.
* **Cashless Authorization Window**: Cashless pre-authorizations at network hospitals are processed within 3 hours upon receipt of complete medical documentation.
* **Reimbursement Turnaround Time**: Reimbursement claims are settled within 18 business days upon receiving all original bills, prescription sheets, and discharge summaries. Delays beyond 35 days incur interest at bank rate plus 1.5% in line with IRDAI regulations.
