# Nova Bank Credit Card Master Policy & Cardholder Agreement

## 1. Credit Card Variants and Annual Membership Fees
Nova Bank issues two primary consumer credit card variants: the **Nova Titanium Credit Card** and the flagship **Nova Apex Metal Credit Card**.

* **Nova Titanium Card**: First-year joining fee of INR 1,500 plus 18% GST. Annual renewal fee of INR 1,500 plus GST is levied at the start of each renewal year. The renewal fee is completely waived if total retail retail purchases exceed INR 1,50,000 within the previous 12 billing cycles.
* **Nova Apex Metal Card**: First-year membership fee of INR 5,000 plus GST, which includes 5,000 bonus welcome points. Annual renewal fee of INR 5,000 plus GST is waived upon reaching an annual spend milestone of INR 5,00,000 in the preceding anniversary year.

## 2. Billing Cycle and Interest-Free Credit Grace Period
* The billing cycle statement is generated monthly on a chosen date (1st, 10th, 15th, or 25th of the month).
* The payment due date is set exactly 20 days after the statement generation date.
* Cardholders who pay their full total statement balance on or before the payment due date enjoy an interest-free grace window ranging between 20 and 50 days, depending on the transaction date relative to the statement cycle.
* The interest-free period does not apply to cash advances (ATM withdrawals), which accrue interest from the date of withdrawal until full repayment.

## 3. Finance Charges (APR) and Minimum Amount Due (MAD)
* **Finance Charges (Interest Rate)**:
  - For Nova Titanium: Finance charges are calculated at 3.50% per month (equivalent to an Annual Percentage Rate [APR] of 42.0% per annum) on revolving balances and unpaid portions.
  - For Nova Apex Metal: Finance charges are discounted to 3.00% per month (effective APR of 36.0% per annum).
* **Minimum Amount Due (MAD)**: MAD is computed as 5% of the total statement balance (or a minimum floor of INR 500, whichever is higher), plus any overdue amounts, late payment charges, and the monthly principal portion of existing EMI installments. Paying only the MAD prevents late reporting to credit bureaus but incurs finance charges on the remaining outstanding balance.

## 4. Late Payment Penalties and Delinquency Tiers
If the Minimum Amount Due is not received by the payment due date, a tiered late payment charge is assessed based on the total outstanding statement balance:
* Outstanding up to INR 500: Nil (no charge).
* Outstanding from INR 501 to INR 5,000: INR 500 plus GST.
* Outstanding from INR 5,001 to INR 10,000: INR 750 plus GST.
* Outstanding from INR 10,001 to INR 25,000: INR 1,000 plus GST.
* Outstanding greater than INR 25,000: INR 1,300 plus GST.
Accounts remaining overdue past 90 days from the due date are classified as Non-Performing Assets (NPA) and reported to RBI-regulated credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax).

## 5. Cash Advances, Foreign Transactions, and Fuel Surcharge
* **Cash Advance (ATM Withdrawal) Fee**: A transaction charge of 2.50% of the withdrawn amount, subject to a minimum charge of INR 500, is levied immediately. Finance charges accrue from the withdrawal date at standard monthly rates.
* **Foreign Currency Markup**:
  - Nova Titanium: A markup fee of 3.50% plus GST is assessed on the converted INR value of international transactions.
  - Nova Apex Metal: Preferential international markup rate of 2.00% plus GST.
* **Fuel Surcharge Waiver**: 1% fuel surcharge is refunded for transactions conducted at all domestic fuel outlets between the ticket sizes of INR 500 and INR 4,000. Maximum surcharge waiver is capped at INR 250 per statement cycle for Titanium cards and INR 500 per cycle for Apex Metal cards.

## 6. Reward Points Accrual and Redemption Policy
* **Titanium Card**: Earns 2 Nova Reward Points per INR 100 spent on general retail purchases, and 4 points per INR 100 spent on dining and airline bookings.
* **Apex Metal Card**: Earns 5 Nova Reward Points per INR 100 on retail, and 10 points per INR 100 on curated international luxury merchants.
* **Point Validity and Value**: 1 Nova Reward Point equals INR 0.25 when redeemed for statement cash credit, or INR 0.50 when redeemed against flights and hotels on Nova Travel Portal.
* Unredeemed reward points expire exactly 24 calendar months from the date of credit. A redemption handling fee of INR 99 plus GST applies per redemption request.

## 7. Card Security, Fraud Reporting, and Zero Liability Protection
* In the event of card loss, theft, or unauthorized card skimming, the cardholder must immediately block the card via the NovaMobile app or call the 24x7 helpline at 1800-209-NOVA.
* **Zero Liability Guarantee**: The cardholder bears zero liability for fraudulent transactions occurring after confirmation of card blocking. For transactions occurring before reporting, cardholder liability is capped at a maximum of INR 1,000 provided the incident is officially reported within 48 hours of occurrence.
