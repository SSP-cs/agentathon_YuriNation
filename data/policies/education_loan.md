# Nova Bank Education Loan Policy Document

## Eligibility

To support students in pursuing higher education both domestically and internationally, Nova Bank offers the Nova Bank Education Loan. The eligibility criteria are as follows:

1. **Age Requirement**: Applicants must be between 18 and 35 years of age at the time of loan application.
2. **Academic Qualification**: A minimum academic score of 60% in the most recent qualifying examination is required.
3. **Approved Institutions**: The program must be recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) or the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) for domestic studies, or equivalent international accreditation bodies for overseas studies.

## Fees and Charges

Nova Bank Education Loan comes with competitive fees and charges designed to be affordable for students and their families:

1. **Processing Fee**: A processing fee of 1% of the loan amount is charged, with a minimum of INR 1,000 and a maximum of INR 10,000.
2. **Prepayment Charges**: Prepayment is permitted after 6 months from the date of disbursement, with a charge of 2% on the outstanding principal.
3. **Late Payment Penalty**: A late payment penalty of 2% per month on the overdue amount will be applied after a grace period of 15 days.

## Limits

The loan limits are structured to accommodate the varying costs of education across different fields and locations:

1. **Domestic Study Loan**: Up to INR 20 lakh for studies within India.
2. **Overseas Study Loan**: Up to INR 40 lakh for studies outside India.
3. **Collateral Requirement**: No collateral is required for loans up to INR 7.5 lakh. For loans above this amount, collateral is required, valued at least 1.25 times the loan amount.

## Timelines

The timelines for processing and disbursement of the Nova Bank Education Loan ensure that students can access funds when needed:

1. **Processing Time**: Loan applications will be processed within 15 business days from the date of submission of complete documentation.
2. **Moratorium Period**: A moratorium period of course duration plus 6 months or 1 year after course completion, whichever is earlier, is provided.
3. **Repayment Term**: The loan repayment term is up to 15 years, starting after the completion of the moratorium period.

## Conditions and Exclusions

To maintain the integrity and sustainability of the Nova Bank Education Loan, certain conditions and exclusions apply:

1. **Interest Rate**: An interest rate of 10% per annum for domestic loans and 12% per annum for overseas loans.
2. **Interest Subsidy**: A 2% interest subsidy is available for students from economically weaker sections with an annual family income below INR 4.5 lakh.
3. **Exclusions**: Loans will not cover non-educational expenses such as travel expenses not directly related to the educational program, personal purchases, or luxury accommodation.

## How to Apply or Claim

Applying for the Nova Bank Education Loan is a straightforward process designed for ease and transparency:

1. **Application Submission**: Applications can be submitted online through the Nova Bank website or at any Nova Bank branch.
2. **Required Documents**: Applicants must submit proof of admission, academic records, proof of income, and identification documents.
3. **Disbursement**: Upon approval, funds will be disbursed directly to the educational institution in up to 3 installments, as per the fee schedule.

## Escalation

In case of any issues or grievances related to the Nova Bank Education Loan, the escalation process is in place to ensure resolution:

1. **Initial Contact**: Contact customer service at 1800-123-4567 available 24/7.
2. **Further Escalation**: If unresolved, escalate to the Branch Manager within 7 business days.
3. **Final Resolution**: If still unresolved, contact the Nova Bank Ombudsman at ombudsman@novabank.com, who will respond within 30 days.

## FAQs

1. **Can I apply for a loan for part-time courses?**
   - No, the Nova Bank Education Loan is only available for full-time courses.

2. **Is there an option to defer the repayment start date?**
   - Yes, repayment can be deferred until 6 months post-course completion or upon gaining employment, whichever is earlier.

3. **What happens if I fail to complete my course?**
   - In the case of non-completion, the loan repayment terms will commence as per the initial agreement, post the moratorium period.
