# Circular NB/2026: Amendments to Motor Insurance

Effective from 1 September 2026, Nova Shield is implementing amendments to the motor insurance policy, specifically altering two existing clauses to enhance customer benefits and policy clarity. These amendments supersede the previous values outlined in our earlier policy documents.

1. **Insured Declared Value (IDV) Calculation**: Previously, for vehicles exceeding 5 years of age, the IDV was negotiated mutually based on an independent surveyor valuation. Now, the IDV for vehicles exceeding 5 years will be calculated using a fixed depreciation rate of 60% on the manufacturer listed selling price, streamlining the process and providing more transparency to policyholders.

2. **No Claim Bonus (NCB) Discount Grid**: Previously, policyholders could earn a 50% NCB discount after 5 consecutive claim-free years, which was the maximum statutory ceiling. Now, the maximum NCB discount has been increased to 55% for 6 consecutive claim-free years, offering further incentive for safe driving practices.

These amendments are designed to ensure our policyholders receive more predictable and beneficial outcomes from their motor insurance policy with Nova Shield. For any questions or further clarifications, please contact our customer service team or your insurance advisor. Thank you for your continued trust in Nova Shield.
