# Nova Shield Home Insurance Policy Document

## Eligibility

Nova Shield Home Insurance is available to homeowners and tenants across India who meet the following criteria:

1. **Property Ownership**: The applicant must legally own or rent the property to be insured. The property's market value should be between INR 25 lakhs and INR 10 crores.
2. **Age Requirement**: The primary policyholder must be aged between 25 and 65 years at the time of policy issuance.
3. **Occupancy**: The property must be occupied for at least 180 days a year. Properties left vacant for over 60 consecutive days are not eligible for coverage.

## Fees and Charges

1. **Premium Payment**: Premiums are based on the insured sum and property location, starting at INR 5,000 annually for properties valued at INR 25 lakhs.
2. **Service Charge**: A service fee of INR 500 is applicable for policy amendments post-issuance.
3. **Late Payment Penalty**: A late fee of 2% of the premium amount is charged for payments delayed beyond the due date, with a grace period of 15 days.

## Limits

1. **Sum Insured**: Home structures can be insured up to INR 10 crores, while contents are covered up to INR 2 crores.
2. **Valuable Items**: High-value items like jewelry and art are covered up to 20% of the total content coverage, with a sub-limit of INR 10 lakhs per item.
3. **Additional Living Expenses**: In case of property damage requiring relocation, coverage includes temporary housing costs up to INR 50,000 per month for a maximum of 6 months.

## Timelines

1. **Policy Activation**: The policy is activated 7 days after the premium payment is received.
2. **Claim Processing**: Claims are processed within 30 days from the date of submission of all required documents.
3. **Renewal**: Policyholders will receive a renewal notice 45 days before the policy expiration date, and renewals must be completed by the expiration date to avoid a lapse.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Natural Disasters**: Coverage includes damages due to earthquakes, storms, and floods, but excludes damages due to gradual land subsidence.
2. **Theft and Vandalism**: Covered, provided the property was secured with standard locks and alarms. Excludes loss due to negligence.
3. **Wear and Tear**: General wear and tear, mold, and pest infestations are excluded from the policy.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **Application Process**: Interested parties can apply online through our website or at our local branches. Required documents include property deeds, identification proofs, and property valuation certificates.
2. **Claim Submission**: Claims can be submitted via our portal or through email within 15 days of the incident. Required documents include photographs of damages, police reports (if applicable), and repair estimates.
3. **Claim Assistance**: Our claim support team is available 24/7 to assist policyholders. Contact them at 1800-123-456 or support@novashield.com.

## Escalation

1. **Initial Resolution**: If a claim or policy issue is unresolved, contact our Customer Service Team at 1800-123-789.
2. **Senior Review**: Unresolved disputes can be escalated to a Senior Manager after 15 days of the initial complaint.
3. **Ombudsman Escalation**: If dissatisfaction persists, policyholders may contact the Insurance Ombudsman within 30 days of the Senior Manager's decision.

## FAQs

1. **Q: How can I increase my coverage limit?**
   - A: Submit a written request for a coverage review. Changes are subject to underwriting approval, with a potential increase in premiums.
   
2. **Q: What happens if I miss my renewal date?**
   - A: If the policy lapses, you must reapply. Coverage will only resume upon approval and payment of the new premium.

3. **Q: Are there any discounts available?**
   - A: Yes, policyholders who bundle home and auto insurance receive a 10% discount on annual premiums. Additionally, installing approved security systems may qualify for a 5% discount.
