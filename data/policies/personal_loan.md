# Nova Bank Express Personal Loan Guidelines & Lending Terms

## 1. Borrower Eligibility and Underwriting Criteria
Nova Bank grants unsecured personal credit facilities under the **Nova Express Personal Loan** program to salaried and self-employed individuals meeting the following criteria:
* **Age Limits**: Minimum age is 21 completed years at application; maximum age is 58 years at loan maturity for salaried applicants and 65 years for self-employed professionals.
* **Income Threshold**: Salaried employees must demonstrate a verifiable net monthly take-home salary of at least INR 30,000. Self-employed applicants must show audited annual gross receipts exceeding INR 6,00,000.
* **Credit Rating**: A minimum TransUnion CIBIL credit score of 720 is mandatory. Applicants with CIBIL scores above 780 qualify for preferential tiered rate discounts of 50 basis points (0.50%).

## 2. Loan Quantum and Flexible Repayment Tenures
* **Sanctioned Amount Limits**: Minimum loan quantum is INR 50,000; maximum credit line is capped at INR 25,00,000, determined by the borrower’s Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio (maximum permissible Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio [FOIR] is 55%).
* **Tenure Options**: Flexible repayment periods span from a minimum of 12 months (1 year) up to a maximum of 60 months (5 years), structured as monthly Equated Monthly Installments (EMIs).

## 3. Interest Rates and Pricing Framework
* **Interest Rates**: Loans are disbursed on a fixed reducing balance method ranging from 10.50% to 18.00% per annum.
  - Category A Corporate Employees: 10.50% to 12.75% per annum.
  - Non-categorized Salaried & Professionals: 13.00% to 16.50% per annum.
  - Emerging Credit Profiles (scores 720 to 749): 17.00% to 18.00% per annum.
* Interest calculation occurs on the declining daily principal balance, with monthly installment deductions via National Automated Clearing House (NACH) or e-Mandate on the 5th of every month.

## 4. Processing Fees, Stamp Duty, and Administrative Charges
* **Processing Fee**: A standard non-refundable processing charge of 1.50% to 2.50% of the gross sanctioned principal, subject to a minimum floor of INR 2,000 and a maximum cap of INR 10,000 plus 18% GST. This fee is deducted directly from the disbursal amount.
* **Documentation and Legal Stamp Duty**: Actual state-specific stamp duty and documentation processing charges ranging from INR 300 to INR 1,200 apply based on the state of execution.
* **Amortization Schedule Fee**: First digital issuance is free; reissuance of physical loan repayment schedule costs INR 250 plus GST.

## 5. Foreclosure, Part-Prepayment, and Lock-In Periods
Borrowers wishing to extinguish or accelerate their debt obligations are subject to clear regulatory and institutional terms:
* **Mandatory Lock-in Window**: No prepayment or total foreclosure is permitted before the completion of 6 consecutive monthly EMI repayments.
* **Foreclosure Charges (Total Prepayment)**:
  - If foreclosed between 7 and 24 months from the loan origination date: A penalty charge of 3.00% of the remaining outstanding principal balance plus 18% GST.
  - If foreclosed after 24 completed EMI payments: Zero prepayment penalty (0% charge) applies for all individual salaried borrowers in compliance with fair practice codes.
* **Part-Prepayment Policy**: Permitted up to 25% of the principal outstanding once per financial year. Part-prepayments made prior to 24 months incur a 2.00% fee on the prepaid amount; post 24 months part-prepayments carry zero fee.

## 6. Default Penalties, Bounced EMIs, and Delinquency Recovery
* **NACH / Cheque Return Charges**: Any dishonor of monthly EMI auto-debit due to insufficient balance incurs a bounce fee of INR 500 plus 18% GST per instance.
* **Penal Interest**: Overdue installment amounts attract penal interest of 2.00% per month (equivalent to 24.0% annualized) from the payment due date until realized in full.
* **Notice of Default**: In the event of 3 consecutive unpaid installments (90 days overdue), the facility is flagged as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) and forwarded to legal recovery channels.

## 7. Documentation and Disbursal Turnaround Time (TAT)
* Required submissions comprise: PAN card, Aadhaar card for biometric e-KYC, last 3 months bank statements showing regular salary credit, and latest 3 salary payslips.
* Pre-approved Nova Bank savings account holders receive instant straight-through digital disbursal into their account within 4 working hours.
* Standard physical applications are evaluated and disbursed within 48 to 72 business hours following physical document verification.
