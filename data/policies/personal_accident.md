# Nova Shield Personal Accident Insurance Policy

## Eligibility

1. **Age Requirement**: Individuals aged between 18 and 65 years are eligible for the Nova Shield Personal Accident Insurance.
2. **Residency Status**: Applicants must be permanent residents of India or hold a valid work visa.
3. **Health Condition**: Applicants must not have any pre-existing conditions that align with our exclusion criteria, with a history of no more than two claims in the past five years.

## Fees and Charges

1. **Premium Rates**: The annual premium for the Basic Tier is INR 5,000, the Premium Tier is INR 10,000, and the Elite Tier is INR 20,000.
2. **Administrative Fees**: A processing fee of INR 500 is applicable for each claim submission.
3. **Cancellation Charges**: A cancellation fee of INR 1,000 applies if the policy is terminated before the completion of the policy term.

## Limits

1. **Death Benefit**: The death benefit is 100% of the sum insured, up to a maximum of INR 50 lakh for the Basic Tier, INR 1 crore for the Premium Tier, and INR 2 crore for the Elite Tier.
2. **Disability Benefit**: 
   - Permanent Total Disability: 100% of the sum insured.
   - Permanent Partial Disability: Up to 50% of the sum insured, depending on the severity.
   - Temporary Total Disability: A weekly benefit of INR 5,000 for Basic, INR 10,000 for Premium, and INR 15,000 for Elite Tier, payable for up to 52 weeks.
3. **Medical Expenses Coverage**: Up to INR 1 lakh for Basic, INR 2 lakh for Premium, and INR 3 lakh for Elite Tier.

## Timelines

1. **Claim Submission Deadline**: All claims must be submitted within 30 days from the date of the accident.
2. **Claim Processing Time**: Claims will be processed within 15 business days from the date of submission.
3. **Policy Renewal Period**: The policy must be renewed 15 days before the expiry date to avoid lapses in coverage.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Exclusions**:
   - Injuries sustained under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
   - Accidents occurring due to participation in hazardous activities such as skydiving or bungee jumping.
   - Self-inflicted injuries or suicide.
2. **War and Terrorism**: Any claims arising from acts of war or terrorism are excluded.
3. **Pre-existing Conditions**: Any disability or injury related to pre-existing conditions as declared at the time of policy purchase is not covered.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **Application Process**: 
   - Complete the online application form and submit the required documents.
   - Pay the applicable premium based on the chosen tier.
   - Receive the policy document via email within 5 business days.
2. **Claim Documents**: 
   - Original medical bills and reports.
   - FIR or police report in case of accidents.
   - Claim form duly filled and signed.
3. **Claim Submission**: Submit all documents via our online portal or mail them to our head office within the stipulated deadline.

## Escalation

1. **Initial Contact**: For any queries, contact our customer support at 1800-123-4567 or email support@novashield.in.
2. **Grievance Redressal**: Unresolved issues can be escalated to the Grievance Officer at grievance@novashield.in, including your policy number and all relevant details.
3. **Final Appeal**: If dissatisfaction persists, a final appeal can be made to the Ombudsman within 30 days of receiving the grievance decision.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. **What is the maximum coverage amount?**
   - The maximum coverage amount is INR 2 crore under the Elite Tier.

2. **How are disability benefits calculated?**
   - Permanent Total Disability benefits are 100% of the sum insured, while Permanent Partial Disability benefits can be up to 50%, based on severity. Temporary Total Disability provides weekly benefits up to 52 weeks.

3. **What is the deadline for claim submission?**
   - Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the accident date.

By adhering to these guidelines and understanding the policy terms, you can ensure that you are well-protected under the Nova Shield Personal Accident Insurance. For further assistance, please contact our customer support team.
