# Nova Bank Debit Card Terms

## Eligibility

Nova Bank offers three variants of debit cards: Silver, Gold, and Platinum, each tailored to different customer needs. Eligibility criteria are as follows:

- **Silver Card**: Requires a minimum monthly balance of INR 5,000 in the associated savings account. Available to customers aged 18 years and above.
- **Gold Card**: Requires a minimum monthly balance of INR 25,000. Designed for customers aged 21 years and above with an active Nova Bank account for at least 6 months.
- **Platinum Card**: Requires a minimum monthly balance of INR 100,000. Exclusive to customers aged 25 years and above with a minimum annual income of INR 1,000,000.

## Fees and Charges

Understanding the fees and charges associated with Nova Bank debit cards helps in managing your finances effectively:

- **Annual Fees**: Silver Card holders incur an annual fee of INR 250, Gold Card holders INR 500, and Platinum Card holders INR 1,000.
- **Replacement Fee**: In case of card loss, a replacement fee of INR 150 applies for Silver, INR 300 for Gold, and INR 450 for Platinum.
- **Foreign Transaction Fee**: A fee of 3% of the transaction amount is charged for international transactions across all card variants.

## Limits

Nova Bank sets specific limits on ATM and Point of Sale (POS) transactions to ensure security and flexibility:

- **ATM Withdrawal Limits**: Silver Card allows a daily limit of INR 10,000, Gold Card INR 25,000, and Platinum Card INR 50,000.
- **POS Transaction Limits**: Silver Card has a daily limit of INR 50,000, Gold Card INR 100,000, and Platinum Card INR 200,000.
- **Contactless Transaction Limits**: Transactions up to INR 5,000 do not require a PIN across all card variants.

## Timelines

Nova Bank ensures prompt service delivery and resolution timelines:

- **Card Issuance**: New debit cards are issued within 7 business days after the application is approved.
- **Replacement Card Delivery**: Replacement cards are delivered within 5 business days from the date of request.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Transaction disputes are typically resolved within 30 days from the date the dispute is reported.

## Conditions and Exclusions

Certain conditions and exclusions apply to ensure secure and fair usage of Nova Bank debit cards:

- **International Use**: Ensure your card is activated for international use by contacting customer service. A 24-hour notice is required for activation.
- **Card Deactivation**: Accounts not maintaining the required minimum monthly balance for three consecutive months may lead to card deactivation.
- **Exclusion from Rewards**: Transactions such as cash withdrawals, balance transfers, and fees are excluded from reward points accumulation.

## How to Apply or Claim

Applying for a Nova Bank debit card or making claims is a straightforward process:

- **Application**: Visit any Nova Bank branch or apply online via the official bank website. Processing takes approximately 5 business days.
- **Report Lost Card**: To report a lost card, call our 24/7 customer service helpline at 1800-123-4567 and immediately block the card.
- **Claim Process**: For disputes or unauthorized transactions, submit a claim form available on our website or at any branch within 15 days of the transaction.

## Escalation

In case of unresolved issues, Nova Bank provides escalation channels to ensure customer satisfaction:

- **Level 1 Escalation**: Contact the branch manager directly within 3 business days if the issue is not resolved by customer service.
- **Level 2 Escalation**: If dissatisfaction persists, escalate to the Regional Head within 7 business days.
- **Final Escalation**: Unresolved issues at the regional level can be escalated to the Banking Ombudsman within 30 days of the initial complaint.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I use my debit card internationally?**  
A: Yes, all Nova Bank debit cards can be used internationally. Ensure you activate international usage by notifying the bank 24 hours in advance.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my daily limits?**  
A: Transactions exceeding daily limits will be declined. You can request a temporary limit increase by contacting customer service.

**Q: How can I check my transaction limits and fees?**  
A: Access your account details, including limits and fees, via our online banking portal or mobile app. Alternatively, contact customer service for assistance.
