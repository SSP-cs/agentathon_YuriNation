# Circular NB/2026: Amendments to Health Insurance

Effective 1 September 2026, Nova Shield Insurance Company announces amendments to the Health Suraksha Comprehensive Plan, superseding the previously stated conditions in the policy document.

1. **Hospital Room Rent and Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Sub-Limits**: Previously, standard room accommodation was capped at 1.00% of the base sum insured per 24 hours of hospitalization. Now, the cap is increased to 1.50% of the base sum insured per 24 hours of hospitalization. This adjustment is made to offer better accommodation options to our insured members, enhancing their comfort during hospital stays.

2. **Cumulative No-Claim Bonus (NCB) Structure**: Previously, the Cumulative No-Claim Bonus could accrue up to a maximum cap of 50% of the original sum insured. Now, the NCB can accrue annually up to a maximum cap of 100% of the original sum insured. This revised structure aims to reward policyholders for maintaining good health and not filing claims, thus encouraging a healthier lifestyle.

These amendments are intended to enhance your coverage experience and provide greater flexibility in managing healthcare needs. All other terms and conditions of the Health Suraksha Comprehensive Plan remain unchanged. For any queries regarding these updates, please contact our customer service team or visit our website.
