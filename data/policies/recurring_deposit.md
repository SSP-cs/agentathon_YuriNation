# Nova Bank Recurring Deposit Policy

## Eligibility

1. **Account Holders**: Nova Bank Recurring Deposit is available to all individuals holding a Nova Bank savings account. Joint account holders are also eligible to open a recurring deposit.
   
2. **Age Requirement**: The primary account holder must be at least 18 years of age. Minors can open a recurring deposit account under the guardianship of a parent or legal guardian.

3. **Residency Status**: The account is available to residents of India. Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) are not eligible to open a recurring deposit with Nova Bank.

## Fees and Charges

1. **Account Setup Fee**: There is no initial setup fee for opening a recurring deposit account with Nova Bank.

2. **Missed Installment Penalty**: A penalty of INR 50 will be charged for each missed installment. This amount will be automatically deducted from the savings account linked to the recurring deposit.

3. **Premature Closure Fee**: If the account is closed before the completion of tenure, a fee of 1% of the total deposit amount will be levied.

## Limits

1. **Minimum Installment**: The minimum monthly installment for a recurring deposit is set at INR 500. Installments must be in multiples of INR 100 thereafter.

2. **Maximum Installment**: The maximum permissible monthly installment is INR 50,000. 

3. **Tenure Options**: The tenure for recurring deposits ranges from a minimum of 6 months to a maximum of 120 months (10 years).

## Timelines

1. **Deposit Schedule**: Installments are to be deposited on the same date each month. If the due date is a non-working day, the installment will be processed on the next working day.

2. **Grace Period**: A grace period of 5 days is provided for installment payments. Post this period, penalties will apply.

3. **Premature Closure Processing Time**: Closure requests are processed within 7 working days from the date of application.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Partial Withdrawal**: Partial withdrawals are not permitted. The account must be closed to access funds before maturity.

2. **Interest Rate**: The interest rate is fixed for the tenure of the deposit, with annual rates ranging from 4% to 6.5%, depending on the tenure selected.

3. **Tax Deduction**: Interest earned is subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) as per prevailing government laws.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **Application Process**: Applications can be submitted through Nova Bank's online banking portal or by visiting any branch. Required documents include identity proof and address proof.

2. **Confirmation and Documentation**: Upon successful creation of the account, a confirmation email and a physical welcome kit will be dispatched within 10 working days.

3. **Maturity Claim**: At maturity, the principal and accrued interest will be credited to the linked savings account within 2 working days.

## Escalation

1. **Customer Service**: For account-related queries, contact the Nova Bank customer service at 1800-123-4567, available 24/7.

2. **Complaint Resolution**: If issues are unresolved, escalate to the Branch Manager. Further unresolved issues can be directed to the Banking Ombudsman after a response period of 30 days.

3. **Feedback and Suggestions**: Customers are encouraged to provide feedback via the Nova Bank mobile app or email at feedback@novabank.com.

## FAQs

1. **Can I change the installment amount mid-term?**
   - No, the installment amount cannot be changed once set. A new account must be opened to change the installment amount.

2. **What happens if I miss multiple installments?**
   - Multiple missed installments will incur cumulative penalties. Three consecutive missed installments may lead to automatic closure of the account.

3. **Is there any auto-renewal option available?**
   - Yes, accounts can be set for auto-renewal. Notification will be sent 30 days prior to maturity for confirmation.

This internal policy document outlines the key aspects of the Nova Bank Recurring Deposit offering. For further details or specific case clarifications, please consult the official policy handbook or contact the policy management team directly.
