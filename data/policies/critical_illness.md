# Nova Shield Critical Illness Cover Policy

Welcome to the Nova Shield Critical Illness Cover Policy. This document outlines the terms and conditions under which Nova Shield Insurance provides coverage for critical illnesses. Our policy aims to offer financial support in times of medical emergencies, allowing policyholders to focus on recovery.

## Eligibility

1. **Age Range**: Individuals aged between 18 and 65 years are eligible to apply for the Nova Shield Critical Illness Cover.
2. **Residency**: Applicants must be residents of India with a valid address proof.
3. **Medical Screening**: Applicants over the age of 50 must undergo a mandatory medical screening at an approved facility.

## Fees and Charges

1. **Premium Payment**: Premiums are payable annually, with options ranging from INR 5,000 to INR 50,000 depending on the sum insured and age bracket.
2. **Processing Fee**: A one-time processing fee of INR 1,000 applies to all new applications.
3. **Late Payment Charge**: A penalty of 2% of the outstanding premium amount is charged for payments not made within the 30-day grace period.

## Limits

1. **Sum Insured Options**: Policyholders can choose from sum insured options of INR 5 lakh, INR 10 lakh, and INR 20 lakh.
2. **Limit Per Illness**: The maximum limit for any single critical illness claim is capped at the sum insured selected.
3. **Annual Coverage Limit**: The total annual claim amount cannot exceed the selected sum insured.

## Timelines

1. **Survival Period**: A survival period of 30 days from the date of diagnosis is required for the claim to be eligible.
2. **Waiting Period**: A waiting period of 90 days from the policy commencement date applies before claims can be made.
3. **Claim Processing Time**: Claims are processed within 15 working days from the date of submission of complete documentation.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Covered Illnesses**: The policy covers 15 critical illnesses, including cancer, heart attack, and stroke.
2. **Pre-existing Conditions**: Pre-existing conditions are excluded from coverage for the first 24 months of the policy.
3. **Lifestyle Exclusions**: Illnesses arising from substance abuse, including alcohol and drugs, are not covered.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **Application Process**: Submit the completed application form along with identity proof, address proof, and medical reports (if required) to the nearest Nova Shield branch.
2. **Claim Submission**: Claims must be submitted with a physician's report, diagnosis details, and relevant medical documentation.
3. **Contact Details**: For application or claim submission, contact our customer service helpline at 1800-123-4567.

## Escalation

1. **Response Time**: Initial grievances are addressed within 7 working days.
2. **Escalation Email**: Unresolved issues can be escalated by emailing escalation@novashield.com.
3. **Final Resolution**: If further action is required, contact our Ombudsman Service, which aims to resolve issues within 21 days.

## FAQs

1. **Can I enhance my sum insured after policy issuance?**
   - Yes, policyholders can enhance their sum insured at policy renewal, subject to underwriting and approval.

2. **What happens if I miss a premium payment?**
   - You have a 30-day grace period to pay the premium. If not paid within this period, the policy will lapse, and coverage will cease.

3. **Are there any tax benefits?**
   - Yes, premiums paid are eligible for tax deductions under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, subject to current tax laws.
