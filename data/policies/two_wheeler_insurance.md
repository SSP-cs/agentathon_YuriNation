# Nova Shield Two-Wheeler Insurance: Internal Policy Document

## Eligibility

1. **Age of Vehicle**: Vehicles up to 15 years old are eligible for coverage.
2. **Vehicle Type**: Insurance is applicable to scooters and motorcycles with an engine capacity ranging from 50cc to 1000cc.
3. **Owner Profile**: Policyholders must be between 18 and 65 years of age and possess a valid driver's license.

## Fees and Charges

1. **Base Premium**: The annual premium starts at INR 1,200 for vehicles under 150cc, increasing to INR 2,500 for vehicles between 150cc and 350cc, and INR 4,000 for vehicles above 350cc.
2. **Third-Party Liability Premium**: A fixed charge of INR 750 is applicable for all vehicles irrespective of engine capacity.
3. **Policy Renewal Fee**: A renewal fee of INR 300 applies if the policy is renewed after the expiry date, within a grace period of 30 days.

## Limits

1. **Own Damage Cover**: Maximum claim limit is set at INR 1,00,000 for vehicles under 150cc, INR 2,50,000 for 150cc to 350cc, and INR 5,00,000 for vehicles above 350cc.
2. **Third-Party Liability**: Coverage up to INR 7,50,000 for property damage and INR 10,00,000 for personal injury or death.
3. **No-Claim Bonus (NCB)**: NCB is capped at 50% for policyholders with no claims over five consecutive years.

## Timelines

1. **Claim Processing**: Claims are processed within 15 working days from the date of submission of complete documentation.
2. **Policy Issuance**: A new policy is issued within 5 working days upon receipt of all necessary documents and premium payment.
3. **Renewal Notice**: Renewal notifications are sent 60 days before the policy expiry date.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Exclusions**: Damage due to war, nuclear risk, or drunk driving is not covered.
2. **Deductibles**: A compulsory deductible of INR 1,000 is applicable for all claims under Own Damage Cover.
3. **Add-Ons**: Zero depreciation cover and engine protect cover are available at an additional premium of INR 500 and INR 750 per annum, respectively.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **Application Process**: Applications can be submitted online or through our authorized agents, requiring proof of identity, vehicle registration certificate, and payment.
2. **Claim Submission**: Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the incident, along with a duly filled claim form, FIR copy (if applicable), and repair estimates.
3. **Documents Required**: Submit a copy of the policy document, driving license, and photographs of the damaged vehicle.

## Escalation

1. **Initial Escalation**: Policyholders can reach out to customer service at 1800-123-4567 for unresolved issues within 5 working days.
2. **Further Escalation**: If unsatisfied with the initial response, escalate to the grievance officer via email at grievances@novashield.com, with a response time of 10 working days.
3. **Final Escalation**: Matters unresolved for more than 30 days can be taken to the Insurance Ombudsman.

## FAQs

1. **What is the maximum NCB I can avail?**
   - The maximum No-Claim Bonus is 50% after five consecutive claim-free years.

2. **Are there any geographical restrictions?**
   - Yes, coverage is limited to the geographical boundaries of India.

3. **Can I transfer my policy to a new owner?**
   - Yes, policy transfer is allowed, subject to a transfer fee of INR 200 and submission of requisite ownership change documents within 14 days of transfer.
