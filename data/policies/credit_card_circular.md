# Circular NB/2026: Amendments to Credit Card

Effective 1 September 2026, Nova Bank is implementing amendments to our credit card policies to enhance benefits for our cardholders. This circular outlines the changes to specific clauses in the Nova Bank Credit Card Master Policy & Cardholder Agreement, superseding previously stated terms.

1. **Foreign Currency Markup for Nova Apex Metal Card**:
   - *Previously*: The Nova Apex Metal Card had a preferential international markup rate of 2.00% plus GST.
   - *Now*: The foreign currency markup for Nova Apex Metal Card transactions is further reduced to 1.75% plus GST. This adjustment makes it more economical for cardholders engaging in international transactions.

2. **Reward Points Accrual for Nova Titanium Card**:
   - *Previously*: Cardholders earned 2 Nova Reward Points per INR 100 spent on general retail purchases and 4 points per INR 100 spent on dining and airline bookings.
   - *Now*: Cardholders will earn 3 Nova Reward Points per INR 100 spent on general retail purchases and 5 points per INR 100 spent on dining and airline bookings. This enhancement provides greater reward accumulation opportunities for our valued Titanium cardholders.

These amendments are part of our ongoing commitment to provide superior value and service to our cardholders. We thank you for your continued trust in Nova Bank and look forward to serving your financial needs with enhanced benefits.
