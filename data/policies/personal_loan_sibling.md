# Nova Bank Flexi Personal Loan Guidelines & Lending Terms

## 1. Borrower Eligibility and Underwriting Criteria
Nova Bank extends unsecured personal credit facilities under the **Nova Flexi Personal Loan** program to both salaried individuals and self-employed professionals who fulfill the following criteria:
* **Age Limits**: Applicants must be at least 22 years old at the time of application; the maximum age limit is 60 years for salaried individuals and 67 years for self-employed professionals at the loan maturity.
* **Income Threshold**: Salaried individuals need to provide proof of a minimum net monthly take-home salary of INR 35,000. Self-employed applicants must have audited annual gross receipts exceeding INR 7,50,000.
* **Credit Rating**: A minimum TransUnion CIBIL score of 710 is required. Applicants with CIBIL scores above 790 are eligible for preferential tiered rate discounts of 60 basis points (0.60%).

## 2. Loan Quantum and Flexible Repayment Tenures
* **Sanctioned Amount Limits**: The minimum loan amount is INR 70,000, with a maximum credit line capped at INR 30,00,000, determined by the borrower’s Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio (maximum permissible Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio [FOIR] is 50%).
* **Tenure Options**: Repayment periods range from a minimum of 18 months (1.5 years) to a maximum of 72 months (6 years), structured as monthly Equated Monthly Installments (EMIs).

## 3. Interest Rates and Pricing Framework
* **Interest Rates**: Loans are offered on a fixed reducing balance method, with rates ranging from 11.00% to 19.00% per annum.
  - Category A Corporate Employees: 11.00% to 13.25% per annum.
  - Non-categorized Salaried & Professionals: 13.50% to 17.00% per annum.
  - Emerging Credit Profiles (scores 710 to 759): 18.00% to 19.00% per annum.
* Interest is calculated on the declining daily principal balance, with monthly installment deductions through the National Automated Clearing House (NACH) or e-Mandate on the 10th of each month.

## 4. Processing Fees, Stamp Duty, and Administrative Charges
* **Processing Fee**: A standard non-refundable processing fee of 1.75% to 3.00% of the gross sanctioned principal, subject to a minimum of INR 2,500 and a maximum of INR 12,000 plus 18% GST. This fee is deducted from the disbursal amount.
* **Documentation and Legal Stamp Duty**: Actual state-specific stamp duty and documentation processing fees ranging from INR 400 to INR 1,500 apply depending on the state of execution.
* **Amortization Schedule Fee**: The initial digital issuance is free; reissuing a physical loan repayment schedule costs INR 300 plus GST.

## 5. Foreclosure, Part-Prepayment, and Lock-In Periods
Borrowers aiming to settle or accelerate their debt obligations are subject to specific regulatory and institutional terms:
* **Mandatory Lock-in Window**: No prepayment or total foreclosure is allowed before the completion of 9 consecutive monthly EMI repayments.
* **Foreclosure Charges (Total Prepayment)**:
  - If foreclosed between 10 and 30 months from the loan origination date: A penalty charge of 3.50% of the remaining outstanding principal balance plus 18% GST.
  - If foreclosed after 30 completed EMI payments: Zero prepayment penalty (0% charge) applies for all individual salaried borrowers in compliance with fair practice codes.
* **Part-Prepayment Policy**: Allowed up to 30% of the principal outstanding once per financial year. Part-prepayments made prior to 30 months incur a 2.50% fee on the prepaid amount; post 30 months part-prepayments carry zero fee.

## 6. Default Penalties, Bounced EMIs, and Delinquency Recovery
* **NACH / Cheque Return Charges**: Any dishonor of monthly EMI auto-debit due to insufficient balance incurs a bounce fee of INR 600 plus 18% GST per instance.
* **Penal Interest**: Overdue installment amounts attract penal interest of 2.50% per month (equivalent to 30.0% annualized) from the payment due date until realized in full.
* **Notice of Default**: In the event of 4 consecutive unpaid installments (120 days overdue), the facility is flagged as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) and forwarded to legal recovery channels.

## 7. Documentation and Disbursal Turnaround Time (TAT)
* Required submissions include: PAN card, Aadhaar card for biometric e-KYC, last 6 months bank statements showing regular salary credit, and the latest 3 salary payslips.
* Pre-approved Nova Bank savings account holders receive instant straight-through digital disbursal into their account within 6 working hours.
* Standard physical applications are evaluated and disbursed within 72 to 96 business hours following physical document verification.
