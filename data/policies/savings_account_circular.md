# Circular NB/2026: Amendments to Savings Account

Effective 1 September 2026, Nova Bank is implementing updates to certain clauses of the Nova Bank Savings Account Policy & Terms of Service. These amendments supersede the previously stated terms and are aimed at enhancing customer experience and aligning with regulatory changes.

1. **Non-Maintenance Penalties for Premium Variant Shortfall**:  
   Previously, if the relationship balance for the Nova Premium Wealth Savings Account dropped below INR 50,000, a flat fee of INR 500 plus 18% GST (totaling INR 590) was debited. Now, this penalty is revised to a flat fee of INR 400 plus 18% GST (totaling INR 472). This adjustment reflects our commitment to providing more favorable conditions for our premium account holders.

2. **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service) Transaction Fees**:  
   Previously, transactions initiated online between INR 1,001 and INR 1,00,000 incurred a fee of INR 5 plus GST, while transactions between INR 1,00,001 and INR 5,00,000 incurred a fee of INR 15 plus GST. Now, transactions between INR 1,001 and INR 1,00,000 will incur a reduced fee of INR 3 plus GST, and transactions between INR 1,00,001 and INR 5,00,000 will incur a fee of INR 10 plus GST. This change is aimed at promoting the use of digital payment methods by reducing transaction costs for our customers.

These amendments are part of our ongoing efforts to optimize our banking services and provide added value to our customers. Thank you for choosing Nova Bank as your trusted financial partner.
