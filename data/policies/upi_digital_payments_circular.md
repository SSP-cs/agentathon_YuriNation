# Circular NB/2026: Amendments to UPI Digital Payments

Effective 1 September 2026, Nova Bank is implementing amendments to our UPI & Digital Payments Governance Policy. These amendments are intended to enhance user convenience and security, and they supersede the previous values stated in our policy document.

1. **UPI Lite Small-Value On-Device Wallet Top-Up Ceiling**: Previously, the wallet could be reloaded up to a cumulative maximum of INR 4,000 per calendar day, with a maximum of 2 top-ups of INR 2,000 each. Now, the top-up ceiling is increased to a cumulative maximum of INR 6,000 per calendar day, allowing a maximum of 3 top-ups of INR 2,000 each. This change aims to provide users with greater flexibility in managing their small-value transactions.

2. **Dispute Resolution Turnaround**: Previously, domestic UPI chargebacks and dual-debit disputes were resolved within 7 business days from receipt of the complaint. Now, these disputes will be resolved within 5 business days, reflecting our commitment to quicker resolution and improved customer service.

These amendments are part of Nova Bank's ongoing efforts to align with regulatory updates and enhance the overall user experience on our digital platforms. Please ensure compliance with the updated provisions. For any queries or further clarification, customers are encouraged to contact our customer service team through the NovaMobile application or Internet Banking portal.

Thank you for your continued trust in Nova Bank's services.
