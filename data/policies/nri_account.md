# Nova Bank NRI Accounts (NRE and NRO) Policy Document

This document outlines the policies and procedures related to Non-Resident Indian (NRI) accounts, specifically Non-Resident External (NRE) and Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) accounts at Nova Bank. This includes eligibility criteria, fees and charges, repatriation rules, interest, documentation requirements, tax deduction at source, and more.

## Eligibility

1. **NRI Status**: Only individuals holding Non-Resident Indian (NRI) status are eligible to open NRE and NRO accounts with Nova Bank. Proof of NRI status, such as a valid passport and visa, is mandatory.

2. **Age Requirement**: The applicant must be at least 18 years of age to open an NRE or NRO account.

3. **Account Types**: Both NRE and NRO accounts can be opened as savings accounts, current accounts, or term deposit accounts. A minimum initial deposit of INR 25,000 is required for savings and current accounts.

## Fees and Charges

1. **Account Maintenance Fee**: An annual maintenance charge of INR 500 is applicable for both NRE and NRO accounts. This fee is waived for accounts maintaining a quarterly average balance above INR 1,00,000.

2. **Transaction Charges**: For NRO accounts, there is a transaction fee of INR 50 per transaction after the first 10 free transactions in a month. NRE accounts offer unlimited free transactions.

3. **Cheque Book Charges**: The first cheque book of 20 leaves is free for both account types. Subsequent cheque books are charged at INR 100 per book.

## Limits

1. **Deposit Limits**: There are no upper limits on deposits into NRE accounts, while NRO accounts can accept up to INR 10,00,000 per financial year from domestic sources.

2. **Withdrawal Limits**: Daily cash withdrawal limits are set at INR 50,000 for both NRE and NRO accounts.

3. **Repatriation Limits**: NRE account balances and interest earned can be freely repatriated. For NRO accounts, repatriation is limited to USD 1 million per financial year, inclusive of taxes.

## Timelines

1. **Account Opening**: The account opening process, upon receipt of all required documents, will be completed within 7 business days.

2. **Repatriation Processing**: Repatriation requests for NRE accounts will be processed within 5 business days, while NRO repatriation requests will take up to 10 business days.

3. **Interest Credit**: Interest on both NRE and NRO accounts is credited quarterly, on the first day of January, April, July, and October.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Interest Rates**: NRE savings accounts earn an interest of 3.5% per annum, while NRO savings accounts earn 4% per annum. Term deposit rates vary from 5.5% to 7% based on tenure.

2. **Tax Deduction at Source (TDS)**: Interest earned on NRE accounts is tax-free. For NRO accounts, TDS of 30% is applicable on interest earned.

3. **Joint Accounts**: NRE accounts can only be held jointly with another NRI. NRO accounts can be held jointly with an Indian resident, but repatriation rules will apply accordingly.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **Application Submission**: Applications can be submitted online through the Nova Bank website or in person at any Nova Bank branch. Required documents include proof of NRI status, a recent photograph, and proof of address abroad.

2. **Document Verification**: Submitted documents will be verified within 3 business days. Applicants will be notified via email regarding the status of their application.

3. **Claim Process for Repatriation**: To initiate a repatriation claim, account holders must submit a repatriation form along with necessary supporting documents, such as Form 15CB and Form 15CA for NRO accounts.

## Escalation

1. **First Level Support**: For any issues related to NRI accounts, customers can contact our NRI customer service desk at +91-800-NOVA-BNK.

2. **Second Level Escalation**: If not resolved, escalate the matter by writing to the NRI Services Manager at nri-services@novabank.com. Expect a response within 3 business days.

3. **Final Escalation Point**: Unresolved issues can be further escalated to the NRI Banking Head via nri-banking-head@novabank.com, with a resolution timeframe of 5 business days.

## FAQs

1. **What is the minimum balance requirement for NRI accounts?**  
   Both NRE and NRO accounts require a minimum quarterly average balance of INR 10,000.

2. **Can funds in NRO accounts be transferred to NRE accounts?**  
   No, direct transfers from NRO to NRE accounts are not permitted.

3. **Is there a penalty for premature withdrawal of term deposits?**  
   Yes, a penalty of 1% on the applicable interest rate is levied for premature withdrawals from both NRE and NRO term deposits.
