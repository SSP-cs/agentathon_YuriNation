# Nova Bank Premium Savings Account Policy & Terms of Service

## 1. Account Variants and Minimum Average Balance (MAB)
Nova Bank offers a distinctive savings account tier: **Nova Premium Savings Account**.

* **Nova Premium Savings Account**: Requires a Monthly Average Balance (MAB) of INR 25,000 in metro and urban branches, or INR 12,500 in semi-urban and rural branch branches. Senior citizens (aged 60 and above) are eligible for a 40% concession on the required MAB (INR 15,000 in metros, INR 7,500 in semi-urban areas).

## 2. Non-Maintenance Penalties
Failure to maintain the mandated Monthly Average Balance attracts a non-maintenance charge assessed on the last calendar day of each month:
* **Premium Variant Shortfall**: If the shortfall is up to 40% of the required balance, the fee is INR 200 plus applicable GST (18%). If the shortfall exceeds 40% of the required balance, the monthly penalty is INR 350 plus 18% GST (totaling INR 413). Under Reserve Bank regulations, an account balance cannot become negative purely due to the levy of non-maintenance charges.

## 3. Interest Rates and Computation Schedule
Interest on Nova Bank savings balances is calculated on a daily end-of-day product basis and credited to the customer account quarterly on the first business day of March, June, September, and December:
* Balances up to INR 15,00,000: Earn interest at the base rate of 3.75% per annum.
* Incremental balances exceeding INR 15,00,000: Earn interest at 4.50% per annum on the incremental amount above INR 15,00,000.
* Balances above INR 60,00,000: Earn 4.75% per annum on the balance portion beyond INR 60,00,000. Interest earnings up to INR 12,000 per financial year are exempt from income tax under Section 80TTA.

## 4. Debit Card Tiers, Limits, and ATM Usage Fees
Nova Bank provides a debit card linked directly to the account variant:
* **Premium MasterCard Titanium Debit Card**: Annual maintenance fee of INR 500 plus GST. Daily domestic ATM cash withdrawal ceiling is INR 75,000; daily Point of Sale (POS) and e-commerce purchase limit is INR 2,00,000.
* **ATM Transaction Allowances**: Customers receive 6 free transactions per month at Nova Bank ATMs. At other bank ATMs, customers receive 4 free transactions per month in the 6 designated metro cities and 6 free transactions in non-metro locations. Subsequent ATM financial transactions are billed at INR 24 plus GST; non-financial inquiries are billed at INR 12 plus GST.

## 5. Fund Transfer Limits and Digital Transaction Fees
Nova Bank encourages digital banking through zero charges on selected electronic channels:
* **NEFT and RTGS**: Completely free and unlimited 24x7 when initiated through Nova NetBanking and the NovaMobile mobile application. Branch-assisted NEFT costs INR 3 to INR 30 depending on transaction value.
* **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)**: Transactions initiated online up to INR 1,500 are free; INR 1,501 to INR 1,50,000 incur a fee of INR 7 plus GST; INR 1,50,001 to INR 6,00,000 incur a fee of INR 20 plus GST.
* **Cash Deposit Limits**: Up to 5 free cash deposits per month or INR 3,00,000 (whichever comes first) at home branches. Cash deposits beyond the limit cost INR 6 per INR 1,000 or a minimum of INR 180.

## 6. Auto Sweep-In Fixed Deposit Facility
Customers can opt into the **Nova SmartSaver Plus Sweep** mechanism:
* The default sweep threshold balance is set at INR 30,000. Any balance in excess of INR 30,000 is automatically swept out in blocks of INR 7,500 into a 450-day auto-renewing fixed deposit earning 7.25% per annum.
* In the event of a withdrawal or payment that exceeds the liquid savings balance, a reverse sweep breaks fixed deposits in units of INR 1,50,000 on a Last-In, First-Out (LIFO) basis with zero penal interest deductions.

## 7. Account Closure Terms and Nomination
* Accounts closed within 10 days of account opening incur no closure charge.
* Accounts closed between day 11 and 10 completed months incur an administrative closure penalty of INR 750 plus GST. No closure fees apply after 10 completed months of tenure.
* Nomination is compulsory at opening unless an explicit, signed physical waiver is submitted. Accounts with registered nominees allow claim settlements within 10 calendar days of receiving death certificate documentation.
