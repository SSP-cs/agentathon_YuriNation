ZENITH COMPREHENSIVE HEALTH & LIFE INSURANCE POLICY (2026 EDITION)

SECTION 1: PREVENTIVE CARE AND WELLNESS BENEFITS
1.1 In-Network Preventive Coverage:
Zenith covers 100% of recognized standard preventive services rendered by participating in-network healthcare providers with zero deductible ($0) and zero coinsurance. This includes annual wellness physicals, routine pediatric immunizations, mammograms, and colorectal cancer screenings as prescribed by federal preventive care mandates.
1.2 Telehealth Access:
Virtual telehealth consultations conducted through Zenith's designated digital platform (ZenithCare App) are covered with a $0 copayment for primary medical triage and behavioral therapy.

SECTION 2: EMERGENCY SERVICES AND HOSPITALIZATION
2.1 Emergency Room Copayment:
Emergency room visits at accredited acute-care hospital facilities incur a standard $250.00 copayment per occurrence. The $250.00 emergency room copayment is waived in the event the insured patient is formally admitted as an inpatient hospital patient within twenty-four (24) hours of initial arrival.
2.2 Non-Emergency Ambulance Transport:
Ground ambulance transportation deemed medically necessary by an attending physician is reimbursed at an 80% coinsurance rate following satisfaction of the individual annual deductible. Elective or non-urgent medical transfers are strictly excluded.

SECTION 3: PRESCRIPTION MEDICATION TIERS
3.1 Formulary Tier Copayments:
Prescription drug benefits follow a four-tier formulary schedule:
- Tier 1 (Preferred Generic Drugs): $10.00 copay per 30-day retail supply.
- Tier 2 (Preferred Brand-Name Drugs): $35.00 copay per 30-day supply.
- Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Specialty Drugs): $70.00 copay per 30-day supply.
- Tier 4 (Complex Biologics & Specialty): Subject to a 20% coinsurance following deductible.
3.2 Mail-Order Prescription Benefit:
A ninety-day (90-day) maintenance supply ordered through the Zenith Central Mail Pharmacy is billed at 2.5 times the single retail 30-day copay.

SECTION 4: EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
4.1 Elective Cosmetic Procedures:
Zenith specifically excludes from coverage all elective cosmetic surgeries, rhinoplasties, dermal fillers, and aesthetic treatments designed primarily to alter physical appearance without restoring impaired bodily functions.
4.2 Reconstructive Surgery Exception:
Cosmetic or corrective surgical interventions are covered exclusively when required to restore physiological function or reconstruct anatomy damaged by severe trauma, congenital disfigurement, or oncological surgical excision, provided prior authorization is approved within twelve (12) months of the incident.

SECTION 5: CLAIMS SUBMISSION TIMEFRAMES AND APPEALS
5.1 Timely Filing Deadline:
All formal reimbursement claims for covered medical services must be submitted by the subscriber or healthcare provider within ninety (90) calendar days from the date services were rendered. Claims received after 90 days will be summarily denied, and providers may not balance-bill the patient.
5.2 Grievance and Expedited Appeals:
Subscribers have the right to file a formal appeal within one hundred eighty (180) days following receipt of an Adverse Benefit Determination. Expedited clinical review decisions involving urgent or life-threatening scenarios must be resolved within seventy-two (72) hours.
