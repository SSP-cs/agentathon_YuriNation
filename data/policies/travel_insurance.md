# Nova Shield Travel Insurance Policy

Welcome to Nova Shield Travel Insurance, designed to provide you with comprehensive protection during your travels. This document outlines the details of our travel insurance plans, including coverage limits, exclusions, claims process, and more. Please review this policy carefully to understand your rights and obligations.

## Eligibility

- **Age Limit**: Our travel insurance is available to individuals aged 18 to 70. Minors aged 0 to 17 can be included in a family plan.
- **Residency Requirement**: The policyholder must be a resident of India and have a valid Indian passport.
- **Travel Duration**: Coverage is available for trips ranging from a minimum of 3 days to a maximum of 180 days.

## Plans and Coverage

Nova Shield Travel Insurance offers three tiers of plans:

1. **Basic Plan**:
   - Medical cover: Up to INR 5,00,000.
   - Trip delay coverage: INR 2,500 per 12-hour delay, up to INR 10,000.
   - Baggage loss cover: Up to INR 20,000.

2. **Standard Plan**:
   - Medical cover: Up to INR 10,00,000.
   - Trip delay coverage: INR 5,000 per 12-hour delay, up to INR 20,000.
   - Baggage loss cover: Up to INR 40,000.

3. **Premium Plan**:
   - Medical cover: Up to INR 20,00,000.
   - Trip delay coverage: INR 10,000 per 12-hour delay, up to INR 40,000.
   - Baggage loss cover: Up to INR 60,000.

## Fees and Charges

- **Premium Payment**: Premiums vary based on the plan selected, duration of travel, and destination. For example, a 30-day trip to Europe under the Standard Plan costs approximately INR 3,500.
- **Processing Fee**: A non-refundable processing fee of INR 250 is applicable for all policy applications.
- **Cancellation Fee**: A fee of INR 500 applies if the policy is canceled more than 48 hours after issuance and prior to the start of the policy term.

## Conditions and Exclusions

- **Pre-existing Medical Conditions**: Coverage for pre-existing conditions is not included.
- **High-risk Activities**: Any claims arising from participation in high-risk activities such as skiing, bungee jumping, or scuba diving are excluded.
- **War and Terrorism**: The policy does not cover incidents related to war, terrorism, or acts of political unrest.

## How to Apply or Claim

- **Application Process**: Applications can be submitted online through our website. Ensure all personal and travel details are accurate and complete.
- **Claim Submission**: Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the incident. Required documentation includes original receipts, medical reports, and police reports (for theft or loss).
- **Claim Processing Time**: Claims are typically processed within 15 working days of receipt of complete documentation.

## Claim Deadlines and Escalation

- **Notification Period**: Notify us of any incident within 24 hours to ensure timely processing.
- **Deadlines**: All claims must be filed within 30 days after the end of the trip.
- **Escalation Process**: If you are unsatisfied with the initial claim decision, you may escalate the matter to our Customer Grievance Cell within 10 days of receiving the decision. Contact us at grievances@novashield.com or call +91-22-5555-7777.

## FAQs

1. **Can I extend my travel insurance policy while abroad?**
   - Yes, you can extend your policy once for an additional 30 days, provided there are no existing claims. Additional premium charges will apply.

2. **Is there a deductible for medical claims?**
   - Yes, there is a deductible of INR 1,000 per claim under the Basic and Standard Plans. The Premium Plan has no deductible.

3. **What should I do in case of a medical emergency?**
   - Contact our 24/7 emergency assistance hotline at +91-22-4444-6666, and we will guide you through the necessary steps.
