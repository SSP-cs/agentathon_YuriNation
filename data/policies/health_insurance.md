# Nova Shield Health Suraksha Insurance Policy Terms

## 1. Plan Coverage Tiers and Sum Insured Matrix
Nova Shield Insurance Company offers retail health coverage under the **Health Suraksha Comprehensive Plan** available across three sum insured choices:
* **Base Sum Insured Tiers**: Option A: INR 5,00,000; Option B: INR 10,00,000; Option C: INR 25,00,000. Coverage is offered on both an Individual and Family Floater basis (covering primary policyholder, legally wedded spouse, and up to 3 dependent unmarried children aged 91 days to 25 years).
* **Entry Age**: Adults from 18 years up to 65 years with lifetime renewability. Children are eligible from day 91 under family floater terms.

## 2. Hospital Room Rent and Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Sub-Limits
Medical room tariffs are governed strictly by percentage ceilings relative to the chosen base sum insured:
* **Standard Room Accommodation**: Capped at 1.00% of the base sum insured per 24 hours of hospitalization (e.g., INR 5,000 per day for an INR 5,00,000 policy; INR 10,00,000 policies have a limit of INR 10,000 per day).
* **Intensive Care Unit (ICU) / ICCU Charges**: Capped at 2.00% of the base sum insured per day (INR 10,000 per day for a 5-lakh sum insured; INR 20,000 per day for a 10-lakh sum insured).
* **Proportionate Tariff Deduction Clause**: If the insured elects a room category with a tariff exceeding the permissible daily ceiling, all associate medical costs (attending physician charges, nursing fees, operation theater expenses, diagnostic labs) will be proportionately reduced by the ratio of (eligible room rent / actual room rent charged). This proportionate deduction does not apply to medicines, consumables, and implants.

## 3. Mandatory Waiting Periods and Specific Disease Restrictions
* **Initial Waiting Period**: 30 calendar days from policy inception. No claims for medical treatment or hospitalization will be honored during this initial month, with the sole exception of emergency treatment arising from direct accidental injury.
* **Pre-Existing Diseases (PED)**: A mandatory waiting period of 36 months (3 consecutive years) of continuous uninterrupted policy coverage is enforced before any claim directly or indirectly linked to diagnosed pre-existing ailments (such as hypertension, diabetes, asthma, thyroid disorders) becomes reimbursable.
* **Specific Named Ailments (24-Month Waiting Period)**: A 24-month (2-year) waiting period applies before coverage commences for: cataract, benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia (all types), hydrocele, anal fistula, piles, stones in urinary or biliary systems, hysterectomy for non-malignant conditions, and joint replacement surgery (unless required due to an accident).
* **Cataract Specific Sub-Limit**: Cataract surgery expense coverage is capped at a strict sub-limit of INR 35,000 per eye, or actual expenses, whichever is lower.

## 4. Inpatient, Pre/Post-Hospitalization, and Day Care Benefits
* **Inpatient Hospitalization**: Covers boarding, nursing, surgeon fees, blood, oxygen, and prosthetic implants provided continuous hospitalization exceeds a minimum period of 24 consecutive hours.
* **Pre-Hospitalization Coverage**: Covers medically documented diagnostics, consultations, and medications for up to 60 days immediately prior to the hospital admission date.
* **Post-Hospitalization Coverage**: Covers relevant follow-up medical expenses, pathology tests, and physiotherapy for up to 90 days following discharge.
* **Day-Care Medical Procedures**: Covers all 541 specified surgical interventions and therapies (such as chemotherapy, radiotherapy, dialysis, eye surgeries) requiring less than 24 hours of hospital stay due to modern technological advancements.
* **Emergency Road Ambulance**: Reimburses up to INR 2,500 per valid hospitalization event for transportation to the nearest equipped medical facility.
* **Organ Donor Hospitalization**: Expenses incurred for organ harvesting surgery on the donor are payable up to INR 2,00,000 or 10% of the sum insured, whichever is lower.

## 5. Cumulative No-Claim Bonus (NCB) Structure
* Policyholders who complete an entire policy year without lodging any claim receive an automatic 10% increase in base sum insured as a Cumulative No-Claim Bonus, without any increase in premium.
* The Cumulative Bonus can accrue annually up to a maximum cap of 50% of the original sum insured (e.g., maximum INR 2,50,000 bonus on a 5-lakh policy).
* If a claim is admitted during any policy year, the accumulated NCB is reduced by 10% at the subsequent renewal, but the base sum insured will never be decreased below the original policy threshold.

## 6. Claim Intimation Timelines and Cashless Settlement Process
* **Planned Hospitalization**: Mandatory intimation must be submitted to Nova Shield Third Party Administrator (TPA) at least 48 hours prior to hospital admission.
* **Emergency Hospitalization**: Intimation must be delivered within 24 hours of hospital admission or prior to final discharge, whichever is earlier.
* **Cashless Authorization Window**: Network hospital cashless pre-authorizations are reviewed and decided within 2 hours of receiving complete medical documentation.
* **Reimbursement Turnaround Time**: Reimbursement claim settlements are disbursed within 15 business days of receiving all original bills, prescription sheets, and discharge summaries. Unjustified delays beyond 30 days attract interest at bank rate plus 2% under IRDAI regulations.
