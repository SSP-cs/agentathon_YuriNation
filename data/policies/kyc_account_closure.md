# Nova Bank KYC and Account Closure Policy

## Eligibility

1. **KYC Compliance**: All account holders must comply with the Know Your Customer (KYC) norms within 30 days of account opening. Failure to do so will lead to account restrictions.
2. **Re-KYC Requirement**: Customers in Tier 2 and Tier 3 categories must undergo re-KYC every 5 years, while Tier 1 customers are required to update their KYC information every 3 years.
3. **Documentation**: Acceptable documents include a government-issued ID, recent utility bill (not older than 3 months), and a passport-sized photograph. For minors, a birth certificate and guardian's ID are required.

## Fees and Charges

1. **Account Closure Charges**: Accounts closed within 6 months of opening incur a fee of INR 500. Accounts closed after 6 months but within 1 year incur a fee of INR 250.
2. **Re-KYC Non-compliance Penalty**: Non-compliance with re-KYC requirements will result in a monthly penalty of INR 100 until the KYC is updated.
3. **Dormant Account Charges**: Accounts that remain dormant for over 24 months will be charged INR 50 per month until reactivated or closed.

## Limits

1. **Transaction Limits**: Dormant accounts have a transaction limit of INR 5,000 per month until reactivated.
2. **Documentation Limit**: A maximum of 5 documents can be submitted for KYC purposes. Any additional documents will require approval from the branch manager.
3. **Closure Requests**: Only one closure request per account is allowed within a 12-month period without additional review.

## Timelines

1. **KYC Processing**: KYC documents will be processed within 7 business days of submission.
2. **Dormant Account Activation**: Re-activation of dormant accounts will be completed within 10 business days upon submission of updated KYC information.
3. **Account Closure**: Account closure requests will be processed within 15 business days from the date of receipt of the completed application.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Exemptions**: Accounts under investigation for fraud or legal issues are exempt from closure until the matter is resolved.
2. **Reactivation Conditions**: Dormant accounts can only be reactivated if all pending charges and penalties are paid in full.
3. **Excluded Accounts**: Accounts with outstanding loans or negative balances cannot be closed until all dues are cleared.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **KYC Update**: Customers can submit their KYC documents via the Nova Bank mobile app, website, or by visiting any branch.
2. **Account Closure**: To close an account, submit a written request either in person at a branch or through the Nova Bank website.
3. **Dormant Account Reactivation**: To reactivate a dormant account, submit updated KYC documents through any of the available channels.

## Escalation

1. **Customer Support**: For any issues related to KYC or account closure, contact Nova Bank customer support at 1800-123-4567, operational 24/7.
2. **Branch Manager**: If unresolved, escalate the issue to the branch manager within 5 business days.
3. **Ombudsman**: Persistent unresolved issues can be taken up with the Banking Ombudsman after a 30-day period from the initial complaint.

## FAQs

1. **What happens if I fail to update my KYC?**  
   Accounts will be restricted, and a penalty of INR 100 per month will apply until KYC information is updated.

2. **Can I reactivate a dormant account online?**  
   Yes, by submitting updated KYC documents via the Nova Bank mobile app or website, but physical verification may be required in some cases.

3. **How do I know if my account is dormant?**  
   Accounts with no transactions for 24 months are classified as dormant. Notifications will be sent via email and SMS before reclassification.
