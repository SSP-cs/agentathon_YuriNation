# Nova Shield Motor Comprehensive Insurance Policy & Add-On Rules

## 1. Scope of Coverage and Insured Declared Value (IDV)
The **Nova Shield Private Car Package Policy** delivers comprehensive protection comprising Section I (Loss or Damage to the Insured Vehicle - Own Damage [OD]) and Section II (Third-Party Liability for property damage and bodily injury under the Motor Vehicles Act).
* **Insured Declared Value (IDV)**: The agreed sum insured for the vehicle is fixed based on manufacturer listed selling price adjusted for age-based depreciation (up to 6 months: 5%; 6 months to 1 year: 15%; 1 to 2 years: 20%; 2 to 3 years: 30%; 3 to 4 years: 40%; 4 to 5 years: 50%). For vehicles exceeding 5 years of age, IDV is negotiated mutually based on independent surveyor valuation.

## 2. Standard Deductibles (Compulsory and Voluntary)
* **Compulsory Deductible**: Every admitted Own Damage claim is subject to an initial compulsory excess borne by the policyholder before claim settlement:
  - Vehicles with cubic engine capacity up to 1500 cc: Fixed compulsory deductible of INR 1,000 per claim.
  - Vehicles with engine capacity exceeding 1500 cc: Fixed compulsory deductible of INR 2,000 per claim.
* **Voluntary Deductible Discounts**: Policyholders choosing a higher voluntary deductible receive an upfront reduction on Section I Own Damage premium:
  - Voluntary deductible of INR 2,500: 20% discount on OD premium (maximum discount capped at INR 750).
  - Voluntary deductible of INR 5,000: 25% discount on OD premium (maximum discount capped at INR 1,500).
  - Voluntary deductible of INR 7,500: 30% discount on OD premium (maximum discount capped at INR 2,000).

## 3. Standard Depreciation Rates on Parts Replacement
In the event of accidental repair or replacement of damaged components under standard policies (without Zero Depreciation riders), the following mandatory depreciation deductions apply:
* **Rubber and Plastic Components**: 50% deduction on rubber parts, tyres, tubes, batteries, nylon parts, and airbags.
* **Fiberglass Components**: 30% deduction on all fiberglass body components.
* **Glass Assemblies**: NIL (0% depreciation) on windshields, windows, and lamp lenses.
* **Metal and Painted Parts**: Deductions determined by vehicle age:
  - Up to 6 months: NIL (0%)
  - 6 months to 1 year: 5%
  - 1 year to 2 years: 10%
  - 2 years to 3 years: 15%
  - 3 years to 4 years: 25%
  - 4 years to 5 years: 35%
  - 5 years to 10 years: 40%
  - Exceeding 10 years: 50%

## 4. Popular Add-On Covers and Endorsements
* **Zero Depreciation (Bumper-to-Bumper) Add-on**:
  - Eliminates all depreciation deductions on metal, plastic, rubber, and glass parts replaced during accidental repair.
  - Eligibility: Vehicles aged up to 5 completed years from the first date of registration.
  - Frequency Cap: Limited to a maximum of 2 claims per policy year. Any subsequent claims within the same policy year are settled under standard depreciation rules.
* **Engine & Electronic Gearbox Protect (Hydrostatic Lock)**:
  - Covers consequential damage to internal engine parts, pistons, connecting rods, cylinder heads, and differential gears caused by water ingestion (hydrostatic lock) or lubricating oil leakage.
  - Mandatory requirement: Policyholder must not repeatedly attempt to crank or restart an engine submerged in floodwaters.
* **24x7 Roadside Assistance (RSA)**:
  - Complimentary emergency breakdown towing up to a radius of 50 kilometers to the nearest authorized workshop.
  - Emergency fuel assistance: Delivery of up to 5 liters of fuel (fuel cost payable by policyholder, delivery free).
  - On-site jump-start, flat tyre replacement, and key lockout support.

## 5. No Claim Bonus (NCB) Discount Grid and Transferability
Policyholders maintaining a claim-free record earn cumulative percentage discounts on the basic Own Damage premium at subsequent renewal:
* 1 consecutive claim-free year: 20% NCB discount.
* 2 consecutive claim-free years: 25% NCB discount.
* 3 consecutive claim-free years: 35% NCB discount.
* 4 consecutive claim-free years: 45% NCB discount.
* 5 consecutive claim-free years: 50% NCB discount (maximum statutory ceiling).
* **NCB Retention and Transfer Rules**: NCB is awarded to the individual policyholder, not the vehicle. When selling the insured car, the policyholder can obtain an **NCB Reserving Certificate** valid for 90 days to apply the accrued NCB discount onto a newly purchased replacement vehicle.

## 6. Claim Intimation Timelines, Surveyor Survey, and Police FIR Mandates
* **Intimation Window**: All incidents of accidental damage, collision, or fire must be reported to Nova Shield claims helpline within 48 hours of occurrence.
* **Survey Inspection**: Digital AI video appraisal or physical independent surveyor inspection is initiated within 24 hours of claim lodgment. The vehicle must not be dismantled or repaired prior to surveyor assessment.
* **Police First Information Report (FIR) Requirements**:
  - Mandatory: A certified copy of the Police FIR is strictly required in claims involving: (a) total vehicle theft or burglary, (b) vandalism or malicious arson, and (c) any road traffic collision resulting in third-party personal injury or fatality.
  - Waived: For minor self-damage accidental collisions without third-party involvement or bodily harm, a signed self-declaration form with geo-tagged photographic evidence is accepted in lieu of an FIR.
