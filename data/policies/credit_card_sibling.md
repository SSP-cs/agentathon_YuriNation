# Nova Bank Credit Card Master Policy & Cardholder Agreement for Nova Platinum Credit Card

## 1. Credit Card Variants and Annual Membership Fees
Nova Bank proudly introduces the **Nova Platinum Credit Card**, a premium offering designed to cater to diverse financial needs.

* **Nova Platinum Card**: Initial joining fee of INR 2,000 plus 18% GST. The annual renewal fee of INR 2,000 plus GST is waived if total retail purchases exceed INR 2,00,000 within the prior 12 billing cycles.

## 2. Billing Cycle and Interest-Free Credit Grace Period
* Billing cycle statements are generated monthly on a selected date (5th, 12th, 18th, or 28th of the month).
* The payment due date is set exactly 25 days after the statement generation date.
* Cardholders who clear their total statement balance in full by the due date benefit from an interest-free grace period ranging from 25 to 55 days, contingent on the transaction date relative to the statement cycle.
* The interest-free period does not apply to cash advances (ATM withdrawals), which incur interest from the withdrawal date until full repayment.

## 3. Finance Charges (APR) and Minimum Amount Due (MAD)
* **Finance Charges (Interest Rate)**:
  - For Nova Platinum: Finance charges are calculated at 2.75% per month (equivalent to an Annual Percentage Rate [APR] of 33.0% per annum) on revolving balances and unpaid portions.
* **Minimum Amount Due (MAD)**: MAD is computed as 4% of the total statement balance (or a minimum floor of INR 400, whichever is higher), plus any overdue amounts, late payment charges, and the monthly principal portion of existing EMI installments. Paying only the MAD prevents late reporting to credit bureaus but incurs finance charges on the remaining outstanding balance.

## 4. Late Payment Penalties and Delinquency Tiers
Failure to pay the Minimum Amount Due by the payment due date results in a tiered late payment charge based on the total outstanding statement balance:
* Outstanding up to INR 600: Nil (no charge).
* Outstanding from INR 601 to INR 6,000: INR 600 plus GST.
* Outstanding from INR 6,001 to INR 12,000: INR 900 plus GST.
* Outstanding from INR 12,001 to INR 30,000: INR 1,200 plus GST.
* Outstanding greater than INR 30,000: INR 1,500 plus GST.
Accounts remaining overdue beyond 75 days from the due date are classified as Non-Performing Assets (NPA) and reported to RBI-regulated credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax).

## 5. Cash Advances, Foreign Transactions, and Fuel Surcharge
* **Cash Advance (ATM Withdrawal) Fee**: A transaction charge of 3.00% of the withdrawn amount, subject to a minimum charge of INR 600, is levied immediately. Finance charges accrue from the withdrawal date at standard monthly rates.
* **Foreign Currency Markup**:
  - Nova Platinum: A markup fee of 3.00% plus GST is assessed on the converted INR value of international transactions.
* **Fuel Surcharge Waiver**: A 1% fuel surcharge is refunded for transactions conducted at all domestic fuel outlets between the ticket sizes of INR 400 and INR 5,000. Maximum surcharge waiver is capped at INR 300 per statement cycle for Platinum cards.

## 6. Reward Points Accrual and Redemption Policy
* **Platinum Card**: Earns 3 Nova Reward Points per INR 100 spent on general retail purchases, and 6 points per INR 100 spent on dining and airline bookings.
* **Point Validity and Value**: 1 Nova Reward Point equals INR 0.20 when redeemed for statement cash credit, or INR 0.40 when redeemed against flights and hotels on Nova Travel Portal.
* Unredeemed reward points expire exactly 30 calendar months from the date of credit. A redemption handling fee of INR 120 plus GST applies per redemption request.

## 7. Card Security, Fraud Reporting, and Zero Liability Protection
* In the event of card loss, theft, or unauthorized card skimming, the cardholder must immediately block the card via the NovaMobile app or call the 24x7 helpline at 1800-209-NOVA.
* **Zero Liability Guarantee**: The cardholder bears zero liability for fraudulent transactions occurring after confirmation of card blocking. For transactions occurring before reporting, cardholder liability is capped at a maximum of INR 800 provided the incident is officially reported within 72 hours of occurrence.
