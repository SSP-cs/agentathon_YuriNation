# Nova Bank Current Account Policy Document

## Eligibility

To open a Nova Bank Current Account, applicants must be at least 18 years old and must provide valid identification documents such as an Aadhaar Card or Passport. The applicant must also provide proof of residence, which can be a utility bill or rental agreement not older than three months. Nova Bank offers three variants of current accounts—Silver, Gold, and Platinum—catering to varying business needs.

- **Silver Account**: Ideal for small businesses with an average monthly balance requirement of INR 25,000.
- **Gold Account**: Suited for medium-scale enterprises, requiring an average monthly balance of INR 75,000.
- **Platinum Account**: Designed for large businesses, with an average monthly balance requirement of INR 1,50,000.

## Fees and Charges

Nova Bank has structured its fees to accommodate different business scales. Charges are applied monthly and vary depending on the account variant.

- **Silver Account**: Monthly maintenance fee of INR 250 if the balance falls below the required average.
- **Gold Account**: Monthly maintenance fee of INR 500 for non-maintenance of the average balance.
- **Platinum Account**: Monthly maintenance fee of INR 750 if the average balance is not maintained.

Additionally, there are transaction charges:
- **NEFT/RTGS Transactions**: Free for all variants up to 100 transactions per month. Subsequent transactions are charged at INR 5 per NEFT and INR 10 per RTGS.
- **IMPS Transactions**: Charged at INR 2 per transaction beyond 50 transactions monthly for all variants.

## Limits

Nova Bank imposes limits on cash deposits to ensure smooth operations and compliance with regulatory standards.

- **Silver Account**: Cash deposit limit of INR 1,00,000 per month. Exceeding this limit incurs a charge of INR 3 per INR 1,000.
- **Gold Account**: Cash deposit limit of INR 2,00,000 per month. Exceeding this limit incurs a charge of INR 2.5 per INR 1,000.
- **Platinum Account**: Cash deposit limit of INR 5,00,000 per month. Exceeding this limit incurs a charge of INR 2 per INR 1,000.

## Timelines

The processing times for account-related services are streamlined for efficiency.

- **Account Opening**: Takes 7 business days from the submission of complete documentation.
- **Cheque Book Issuance**: Dispatched within 5 business days upon request.
- **Overdraft Facility Approval**: Processed within 10 business days, subject to eligibility and documentation.

## Conditions and Exclusions

Certain conditions and exclusions apply to the Nova Bank Current Account.

- **Overdraft Facility**: Available only to Gold and Platinum account holders. Silver account holders are not eligible.
- **Overdraft Limit**: Gold account holders can avail up to INR 1,00,000, while Platinum account holders can avail up to INR 2,50,000.
- **Exclusions**: The overdraft facility is not applicable for accounts with an average balance shortfall for more than two consecutive months.

## How to Apply or Claim

Applicants can apply for a Nova Bank Current Account through various channels.

- **Online**: Visit the Nova Bank website, fill out the application form, and upload necessary documents.
- **Branch Visit**: Complete the application form and submit documents at any Nova Bank branch.
- **Customer Care**: Call the Nova Bank customer care at 1800-100-1234 for guidance on account opening.

## Escalation

If applicants face issues during the application process, they can escalate the matter as follows:

1. **First Level**: Contact branch manager within 5 working days of the issue.
2. **Second Level**: Email the regional office at regionaloffice@novabank.com if unresolved within 10 working days.
3. **Third Level**: Write to the Head of Customer Service at headcusservice@novabank.com if no resolution is received within 20 working days.

## FAQs

- **What happens if I fail to maintain the average balance?**
  - A maintenance fee of INR 250, INR 500, or INR 750 is charged monthly, depending on your account type.

- **Can I upgrade my account?**
  - Yes, account upgrades can be requested. Processing takes 10 business days, provided the average balance criteria of the new variant have been met for three consecutive months.

- **What is the process for resolving transaction disputes?**
  - Report disputes to customer care within 30 days of the transaction. Resolution is typically provided within 15 business days.

This policy ensures Nova Bank's current account offerings are transparent, efficient, and cater to the financial needs of businesses at various scales.
