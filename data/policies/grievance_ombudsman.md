# Nova Bank Grievance Redressal and Escalation Policy

## Eligibility

1. **Account Holders**: All individuals holding active accounts with Nova Bank, including savings, current, and loan accounts, are eligible to file complaints.
2. **Authorized Representatives**: Complaints can also be lodged by authorized representatives of account holders, provided proper authorization documents are submitted.
3. **Service Users**: Individuals utilizing Nova Bank's products or services, such as credit card users or loan applicants, are eligible for grievance redressal.

## Fees and Charges

1. **No Filing Fee**: Nova Bank does not charge any fee for filing a grievance at any level of the complaint process.
2. **Compensation**: In case of delayed resolution beyond stipulated timelines, a compensation of INR 200 per day may be awarded, subject to a maximum of INR 5,000.
3. **Escalation Costs**: Escalation to the Banking Ombudsman is free of charge, ensuring accessibility to all customers.

## Limits

1. **Compensation Ceiling**: The maximum compensatory amount that can be claimed for grievances is capped at INR 50,000, except in cases of fraud or unauthorized transactions.
2. **Transaction Limit**: Complaints related to transactions exceeding INR 1,00,000 will be prioritized and addressed with urgency.
3. **Service Scope**: Grievances can be related to banking services including, but not limited to, transaction failures, unauthorized transactions, and service delays.

## Timelines

1. **Level 1: Branch Level Resolution**: Complaints should be resolved within 7 working days. If unresolved, they will escalate automatically to the next level.
2. **Level 2: Regional Office Level Resolution**: Resolution must be provided within 15 working days from escalation.
3. **Level 3: Head Office Escalation**: Final resolution should be achieved within 30 working days from the date of original complaint filing.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Documentation Requirement**: All complaints must be supported by relevant documentation such as transaction receipts or correspondence copies.
2. **Exclusion of Third-party Apps**: Grievances related to third-party applications linked to Nova Bank services are excluded from this policy.
3. **Transaction Age Limit**: Only transactions that have occurred within the last 12 months are eligible for grievance filing.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **Online Submission**: Complaints can be submitted via the Nova Bank website or mobile app. A complaint reference number will be generated within 24 hours.
2. **In-person Submission**: Customers can visit any branch to file a complaint. Acknowledgment will be provided within 2 working days.
3. **Email Communication**: Complaints can be sent via email to grievances@novabank.com. Response and acknowledgment will be sent within 3 working days.

## Escalation

1. **Ombudsman Escalation**: If the grievance is unresolved after 30 days, customers can escalate the issue to the Banking Ombudsman. Contact details are available on the Nova Bank website.
2. **Internal Escalation**: Unresolved complaints at the branch level after 7 days will automatically escalate to the regional office.
3. **Priority Escalation**: Complaints involving amounts above INR 1,00,000 or potential fraud are escalated directly to Level 2 for immediate attention.

## FAQs

1. **What should I do if my complaint is not addressed within the timeline?**  
   If your complaint is not resolved within the specified timeline, it will automatically escalate to the next level. You can also contact our helpline at 1800-123-4567 for assistance.

2. **How will I receive compensation?**  
   Approved compensations will be credited directly to your linked Nova Bank account within 10 working days of resolution confirmation.

3. **Can I file a complaint for a transaction made a year ago?**  
   No, grievances can only be filed for transactions that occurred within the last 12 months. Transactions older than this period are not eligible for complaint filing.
