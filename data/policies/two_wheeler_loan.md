# Nova Bank Two-Wheeler Loan Policy Document

## Eligibility

To qualify for a Nova Bank Two-Wheeler Loan, applicants must meet the following criteria:

1. **Age**: Applicants must be between 21 and 60 years of age at the time of loan maturity.
2. **Income**: Minimum net monthly income should be INR 15,000 for salaried individuals and INR 20,000 for self-employed individuals.
3. **Employment**: Salaried individuals must have a minimum of one year of continuous employment with their current employer. Self-employed individuals should have been in their current business for at least two years.

## Fees and Charges

Nova Bank ensures transparency in all fees and charges associated with the Two-Wheeler Loan:

1. **Processing Fee**: A non-refundable processing fee of 2.5% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of INR 1,500 and a maximum of INR 5,000, is payable at the time of loan application.
2. **Prepayment Charges**: Prepayment is allowed after 6 months from the date of disbursement, with a prepayment charge of 4% on the principal outstanding.
3. **Late Payment Penalty**: A penalty of 2% per month on the overdue installment amount will be charged in case of late payments.

## Limits

Nova Bank provides financial flexibility with the following loan limits:

1. **Loan Amount**: The minimum loan amount is INR 20,000, while the maximum amount is INR 1,50,000.
2. **Funding Percentage**: Up to 85% of the ex-showroom price of the two-wheeler can be financed.
3. **Tenure**: Loan tenure options range from 12 months to 48 months, allowing customers to choose a repayment schedule that fits their financial planning.

## Timelines

Efficient processing ensures timely access to funds:

1. **Loan Approval**: Loan applications are processed within 3 working days from the date of submission of complete documents.
2. **Disbursement**: Once approved, loan disbursement occurs within 2 working days.
3. **Document Processing**: All necessary documentation must be submitted within 15 days of loan application to avoid any delays.

## Conditions and Exclusions

Certain conditions and exclusions apply to the Nova Bank Two-Wheeler Loan:

1. **Eligible Vehicles**: Only new two-wheelers are eligible for financing; used or second-hand vehicles are excluded.
2. **Credit Score**: A minimum CIBIL score of 700 is required for loan approval.
3. **Geographical Limitations**: Loans are available only for vehicles purchased and registered within the state where the branch is located.

## How to Apply or Claim

Applying for a Nova Bank Two-Wheeler Loan is a streamlined process:

1. **Application Submission**: Applicants can submit their loan application online via the Nova Bank website or in-person at any branch.
2. **Documentation**: Required documents include proof of identity, address proof, income proof, and vehicle quotation.
3. **Assistance**: For assistance, applicants can contact customer service at 1800-123-4567 or email twowheelerloans@novabank.com.

## Escalation

For issues or grievances related to the Two-Wheeler Loan, customers can escalate as follows:

1. **First Level**: Contact the Branch Manager at the branch where the loan was applied.
2. **Second Level**: If unresolved, escalate to the Regional Loan Officer via email at regionalloanofficer@novabank.com.
3. **Final Level**: For further escalation, reach out to the Nova Bank Ombudsman at ombudsman@novabank.com. Responses are provided within 10 working days.

## FAQs

**1. Can I apply for a Two-Wheeler Loan if I am self-employed?**  
Yes, self-employed individuals can apply, provided they have a minimum net monthly income of INR 20,000 and have been in business for at least two years.

**2. What is the maximum tenure available for repayment?**  
The maximum tenure for a Nova Bank Two-Wheeler Loan is 48 months.

**3. Are prepayment options available without any charges?**  
Prepayment is available after 6 months with a 4% charge on the principal outstanding.

By adhering to these guidelines, Nova Bank aims to provide a seamless and efficient experience for all customers seeking a Two-Wheeler Loan. For further inquiries, please contact our customer service team.
