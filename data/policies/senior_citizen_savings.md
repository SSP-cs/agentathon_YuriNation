# Nova Bank Senior Citizen Savings Account Policy

## Eligibility

1. **Age Criteria**: The Nova Bank Senior Citizen Savings Account is exclusively available for individuals aged 60 years and above. Proof of age, such as a birth certificate or government-issued ID, must be provided at the time of application.
2. **Account Holders**: Joint accounts can be opened if at least one account holder meets the age requirement. The primary account holder must be a senior citizen.
3. **Residency Requirement**: This account is available to residents of India. Non-resident Indians (NRIs) are not eligible for this product.

## Fees and Charges

1. **Maintenance Fee**: There is no monthly maintenance fee for the Senior Citizen Savings Account. 
2. **Minimum Balance Requirement**: Account holders are required to maintain a minimum balance of INR 2,500. Failure to maintain this balance will result in a penalty of INR 100 per month.
3. **Transaction Charges**: Up to 10 free cash withdrawals are permitted per month; subsequent withdrawals will incur a fee of INR 20 per transaction.

## Limits

1. **Deposit Limits**: There is no maximum limit on deposits. However, cash deposits exceeding INR 10,00,000 in a financial year require additional documentation as per regulatory guidelines.
2. **Withdrawal Limits**: A daily withdrawal limit of INR 50,000 is applicable for cash withdrawals from ATMs.
3. **Transaction Limits**: The account supports up to INR 2,00,000 per day for online transactions, with a monthly cap of INR 20,00,000.

## Timelines

1. **Account Approval**: Account opening requests are processed within 5 working days upon receipt of complete documentation.
2. **Interest Payment**: Interest is credited to the account on the last day of each quarter: March, June, September, and December.
3. **Nomination Processing**: Nominations can be added or modified within 3 working days upon submission of the required form.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Interest Rate**: Senior citizen accounts earn an additional interest of 0.5% over the standard savings account rate, which is currently 4% per annum.
2. **Free Services**: Account holders are entitled to a complimentary annual checkbook (up to 25 leaves) and a free debit card.
3. **Exclusions**: This account does not offer overdraft facilities. Additionally, it cannot be used as a lien for loans or credit facilities from Nova Bank.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **Application Process**: Prospective customers can apply for the Senior Citizen Savings Account by visiting any Nova Bank branch with valid identification and proof of age.
2. **Required Documents**: Applicants must provide a completed application form, a photo ID, and a recent passport-sized photograph.
3. **Nomination**: Nominees can be added at the time of account opening or later by filling out the 'Nomination Form DA1' available at all branches.

## Escalation

1. **First Level Support**: For any queries or issues, customers can contact Nova Bank’s customer service at 1800-123-4567 or email support@novabank.com.
2. **Escalation Point**: If the issue is not resolved within 7 working days, it can be escalated to the Branch Manager.
3. **Final Escalation**: Unresolved issues after 15 working days can be escalated to the Banking Ombudsman at the Reserve Bank of India.

## FAQs

1. **What is the interest rate for the Senior Citizen Savings Account?**  
   The account offers an additional interest rate of 0.5% over the standard rate of 4%, resulting in an effective rate of 4.5% per annum.

2. **How many free ATM transactions are allowed?**  
   Account holders can make up to 10 free cash withdrawals per month from Nova Bank ATMs.

3. **Can I add a nominee to my account?**  
   Yes, you can add a nominee at any time by submitting the 'Nomination Form DA1'. The process takes up to 3 working days.

This policy document outlines the features and conditions of the Nova Bank Senior Citizen Savings Account, providing essential information for eligible customers to manage their accounts effectively.
