# Nova Shield Motor Third-Party Plus Policy & Add-On Rules

## 1. Scope of Coverage and Insured Declared Value (IDV)
The **Nova Shield Motor Third-Party Plus Policy** provides comprehensive third-party liability protection along with limited own damage cover for the insured vehicle. It includes Section I (Third-Party Liability for property damage and bodily injury under the Motor Vehicles Act) and Section II (Limited Own Damage [OD] Coverage).
* **Insured Declared Value (IDV)**: The agreed sum insured for the vehicle is calculated based on manufacturer listed selling price adjusted for age-related depreciation (up to 6 months: 4%; 6 months to 1 year: 12%; 1 to 2 years: 18%; 2 to 3 years: 28%; 3 to 4 years: 38%; 4 to 5 years: 48%). For vehicles older than 5 years, IDV is negotiated based on an independent expert's valuation.

## 2. Standard Deductibles (Compulsory and Voluntary)
* **Compulsory Deductible**: Each eligible Own Damage claim is subject to an initial compulsory deductible paid by the policyholder before claim processing:
  - Vehicles with cubic engine capacity up to 1500 cc: Fixed compulsory deductible of INR 1,200 per claim.
  - Vehicles with engine capacity exceeding 1500 cc: Fixed compulsory deductible of INR 2,400 per claim.
* **Voluntary Deductible Discounts**: Policyholders opting for a higher voluntary deductible are entitled to an upfront reduction on Section II Own Damage premium:
  - Voluntary deductible of INR 3,000: 18% discount on OD premium (maximum discount capped at INR 800).
  - Voluntary deductible of INR 6,000: 23% discount on OD premium (maximum discount capped at INR 1,400).
  - Voluntary deductible of INR 8,000: 28% discount on OD premium (maximum discount capped at INR 1,800).

## 3. Standard Depreciation Rates on Parts Replacement
In cases of accidental repair or replacement of damaged parts under standard policies (without Zero Depreciation add-ons), the following mandatory depreciation deductions apply:
* **Rubber and Plastic Components**: 48% deduction on rubber parts, tyres, tubes, batteries, nylon parts, and airbags.
* **Fiberglass Components**: 28% deduction on all fiberglass body components.
* **Glass Assemblies**: NIL (0% depreciation) on windshields, windows, and lamp lenses.
* **Metal and Painted Parts**: Deductions based on vehicle age:
  - Up to 6 months: NIL (0%)
  - 6 months to 1 year: 6%
  - 1 year to 2 years: 12%
  - 2 years to 3 years: 16%
  - 3 years to 4 years: 24%
  - 4 years to 5 years: 34%
  - 5 years to 10 years: 42%
  - Exceeding 10 years: 52%

## 4. Popular Add-On Covers and Endorsements
* **Zero Depreciation (Bumper-to-Bumper) Add-on**:
  - Removes all depreciation deductions on metal, plastic, rubber, and glass parts replaced during accidental repair.
  - Eligibility: Vehicles aged up to 6 completed years from the first date of registration.
  - Frequency Cap: Limited to a maximum of 3 claims per policy year. Additional claims within the same policy year are processed under standard depreciation rules.
* **Engine & Electronic Gearbox Protect (Hydrostatic Lock)**:
  - Covers consequential damage to internal engine parts, pistons, connecting rods, cylinder heads, and differential gears caused by water ingress (hydrostatic lock) or lubricating oil leakage.
  - Mandatory requirement: Policyholder must avoid repeated attempts to start an engine submerged in floodwaters.
* **24x7 Roadside Assistance (RSA)**:
  - Complimentary emergency breakdown towing up to a radius of 60 kilometers to the nearest authorized workshop.
  - Emergency fuel assistance: Delivery of up to 6 liters of fuel (fuel cost payable by policyholder, delivery free).
  - On-site jump-start, flat tyre replacement, and key lockout services.

## 5. No Claim Bonus (NCB) Discount Grid and Transferability
Policyholders maintaining a claim-free record earn cumulative percentage discounts on the basic Own Damage premium at subsequent renewal:
* 1 consecutive claim-free year: 18% NCB discount.
* 2 consecutive claim-free years: 23% NCB discount.
* 3 consecutive claim-free years: 33% NCB discount.
* 4 consecutive claim-free years: 43% NCB discount.
* 5 consecutive claim-free years: 53% NCB discount (above statutory ceiling).
* **NCB Retention and Transfer Rules**: NCB is awarded to the individual policyholder, not the vehicle. Upon selling the insured vehicle, the policyholder can obtain an **NCB Reserving Certificate** valid for 100 days to apply the accrued NCB discount to a newly purchased vehicle.

## 6. Claim Intimation Timelines, Surveyor Survey, and Police FIR Mandates
* **Intimation Window**: All incidents of accidental damage, collision, or fire must be reported to the Nova Shield claims helpline within 72 hours of occurrence.
* **Survey Inspection**: Digital AI video appraisal or physical independent surveyor inspection is initiated within 48 hours of claim lodgment. The vehicle must not be dismantled or repaired before surveyor assessment.
* **Police First Information Report (FIR) Requirements**:
  - Mandatory: A certified copy of the Police FIR is strictly required for claims involving: (a) total vehicle theft or burglary, (b) vandalism or malicious arson, and (c) any road traffic collision resulting in third-party personal injury or fatality.
  - Waived: For minor self-damage accidental collisions without third-party involvement or bodily harm, a signed self-declaration form with geo-tagged photographic evidence is accepted instead of an FIR.
