# Nova Bank Savings Account Policy & Terms of Service

## 1. Account Variants and Minimum Average Balance (MAB)
Nova Bank offers two retail savings account tiers: **Nova Classic Savings Account** and **Nova Premium Wealth Savings Account**.

* **Nova Classic Savings Account**: Requires a Monthly Average Balance (MAB) of INR 10,000 in metro and urban branches, or INR 5,000 in semi-urban and rural branch branches. Senior citizens (aged 60 and above) are eligible for a 50% concession on the required MAB (INR 5,000 in metros, INR 2,500 in semi-urban areas).
* **Nova Premium Wealth Savings Account**: Requires a unified MAB of INR 50,000 across all branch locations, or a Total Relationship Value (TRV) of INR 5,00,000 spanning fixed deposits and mutual fund holdings.

## 2. Non-Maintenance Penalties
Failure to maintain the mandated Monthly Average Balance attracts a non-maintenance charge assessed on the last calendar day of each month:
* **Classic Variant Shortfall**: If the shortfall is up to 50% of the required balance, the fee is INR 150 plus applicable GST (18%). If the shortfall exceeds 50% of the required balance, the monthly penalty is INR 250 plus 18% GST (totaling INR 295).
* **Premium Variant Shortfall**: If the relationship balance falls below INR 50,000, a flat fee of INR 500 plus 18% GST (totaling INR 590) is debited. Under Reserve Bank regulations, an account balance cannot become negative purely due to the levy of non-maintenance charges.

## 3. Interest Rates and Computation Schedule
Interest on Nova Bank savings balances is calculated on a daily end-of-day product basis and credited to the customer account quarterly on the first business day of July, October, January, and April:
* Balances up to INR 10,00,000: Earn interest at the base rate of 3.50% per annum.
* Incremental balances exceeding INR 10,00,000: Earn interest at 4.00% per annum on the incremental amount above INR 10,00,000.
* Balances above INR 50,00,000: Earn 4.25% per annum on the balance portion beyond INR 50,00,000. Interest earnings up to INR 10,000 per financial year are exempt from income tax under Section 80TTA.

## 4. Debit Card Tiers, Limits, and ATM Usage Fees
Nova Bank provides debit cards linked directly to account variants:
* **Classic RuPay Platinum Debit Card**: Annual maintenance fee of INR 300 plus GST. Daily domestic ATM cash withdrawal ceiling is INR 50,000; daily Point of Sale (POS) and e-commerce purchase limit is INR 1,00,000.
* **Premium Visa Signature Debit Card**: Annual maintenance fee of INR 750 plus GST (waived for primary account holders maintaining TRV above INR 10,00,000). Daily ATM cash withdrawal limit is INR 1,00,000; daily POS and online transaction ceiling is INR 3,00,000.
* **ATM Transaction Allowances**: Customers receive 5 free transactions per month at Nova Bank ATMs. At other bank ATMs, customers receive 3 free transactions per month in the 6 designated metro cities (Mumbai, New Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad) and 5 free transactions in non-metro locations. Subsequent ATM financial transactions are billed at INR 21 plus GST; non-financial inquiries are billed at INR 10 plus GST.

## 5. Fund Transfer Limits and Digital Transaction Fees
Nova Bank encourages digital banking through zero charges on selected electronic channels:
* **NEFT and RTGS**: Completely free and unlimited 24x7 when initiated through Nova NetBanking and the NovaMobile mobile application. Branch-assisted NEFT costs INR 2.50 to INR 25 depending on transaction value.
* **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)**: Transactions initiated online up to INR 1,000 are free; INR 1,001 to INR 1,00,000 incur a fee of INR 5 plus GST; INR 1,00,001 to INR 5,00,000 incur a fee of INR 15 plus GST.
* **Cash Deposit Limits**: Up to 4 free cash deposits per month or INR 2,00,000 (whichever comes first) at home branches. Cash deposits beyond the limit cost INR 5 per INR 1,000 or a minimum of INR 150.

## 6. Auto Sweep-In Fixed Deposit Facility
Customers can opt into the **Nova SmartSaver Sweep** mechanism:
* The default sweep threshold balance is set at INR 25,000. Any balance in excess of INR 25,000 is automatically swept out in blocks of INR 5,000 into a 390-day auto-renewing fixed deposit earning 6.75% per annum.
* In the event of a withdrawal or payment that exceeds the liquid savings balance, a reverse sweep breaks fixed deposits in units of INR 1,00,00 on a Last-In, First-Out (LIFO) basis with zero penal interest deductions.

## 7. Account Closure Terms and Nomination
* Accounts closed within 14 days of account opening incur no closure charge.
* Accounts closed between day 15 and 12 completed months incur an administrative closure penalty of INR 500 plus GST. No closure fees apply after 12 completed months of tenure.
* Nomination is compulsory at opening unless an explicit, signed physical waiver is submitted. Accounts with registered nominees allow claim settlements within 15 calendar days of receiving death certificate documentation.
