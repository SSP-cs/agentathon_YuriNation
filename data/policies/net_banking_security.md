# Nova Bank Net Banking and Mobile Security Policy

The Nova Bank Net Banking and Mobile Security Policy outlines the rules and guidelines for using our digital channels, ensuring secure and effective banking experiences for our customers. This policy covers login limits, OTP validity, fraud reporting windows, and liability rules.

## Eligibility

1. **Account Holders**: Only individuals with active Nova Bank accounts can register for net banking and mobile banking services. A minimum balance of INR 1,000 is required for eligibility.
   
2. **Age Requirement**: Customers must be at least 18 years old to register for net banking services. Minor account holders (ages 13-17) can access mobile banking with parental consent.

3. **Service Tiers**: Nova Bank offers three tiers of digital banking services—Basic, Advanced, and Premium. Eligibility for the Advanced and Premium tiers requires maintaining an average quarterly balance of INR 25,000 and INR 1,00,000, respectively.

## Fees and Charges

1. **Registration Fee**: There is no registration fee for net banking or mobile banking services at the Basic tier. The Advanced tier incurs a monthly service charge of INR 50, while the Premium tier incurs INR 100.

2. **Transaction Fees**: Online fund transfers via NEFT are free for all tiers. IMPS transactions above INR 10,000 are charged at INR 5 per transaction for Basic tier users.

3. **SMS Alerts**: SMS alert services are free for the first 20 alerts per month. Each additional alert is charged at INR 1 for Basic tier users. Advanced and Premium users receive unlimited free alerts.

## Limits

1. **Login Attempts**: Users are allowed a maximum of three consecutive failed login attempts before their account is temporarily locked. Lock duration is 24 hours for Basic, 12 hours for Advanced, and 6 hours for Premium users.

2. **Transaction Limits**: Basic tier users have a daily transaction limit of INR 50,000. Advanced users can transact up to INR 2,00,000 per day, while Premium users have a limit of INR 5,00,000.

3. **OTP Validity**: One-Time Passwords (OTPs) are valid for 5 minutes across all service tiers and can be used only once per transaction.

## Timelines

1. **Registration Processing**: New registrations for net banking and mobile banking services are processed within 2 business days.

2. **Fraud Reporting Window**: Customers must report any unauthorized transaction within 3 days from the transaction date to ensure limited liability.

3. **Dispute Resolution**: The resolution of disputes related to unauthorized transactions will be completed within 10 business days from the date of reporting.

## Conditions and Exclusions

1. **Device Security**: Users must ensure their devices meet Nova Bank’s security standards, including up-to-date antivirus software and operating system patches.

2. **Exclusions**: Nova Bank is not liable for unauthorized transactions resulting from user negligence, such as sharing passwords or OTPs.

3. **Account Inactivity**: Accounts inactive for 90 days will be temporarily suspended. Reactivation requires verification and may take up to 2 business days.

## How to Apply or Claim

1. **Application Process**: Customers can register for net banking and mobile banking services through our website or mobile app. Required documents include a valid ID and proof of address.

2. **Claiming Unauthorized Transactions**: To claim a refund for unauthorized transactions, users must fill out a dispute form available on our website or app and submit it within the fraud reporting window.

3. **Verification**: Claims processing requires identity verification, which may include submitting additional documentation or security questions.

## Escalation

1. **Initial Support**: For immediate assistance, users should contact our 24/7 helpline at 1800-123-4567.

2. **Grievance Redressal**: If issues persist, escalate the matter by emailing grievance@novabank.co.in. Expect a response within 2 business days.

3. **Ombudsman**: If not satisfied with the resolution, users can approach the Banking Ombudsman within 30 days of receiving the final response from Nova Bank.

## FAQs

1. **Can I reset my net banking password online?**
   - Yes, users can reset their passwords online. A temporary password is sent via SMS and email, valid for 24 hours.

2. **What happens if I exceed my daily transaction limit?**
   - Transactions exceeding the daily limit are automatically declined. Users can request a temporary limit increase by contacting customer support.

3. **How do I activate SMS alerts?**
   - SMS alerts can be activated through the mobile app or by sending an SMS with the keyword "ALERT" to 56789. Activation is confirmed within 1 business day.

This policy is designed to protect our customers and ensure a seamless and secure banking experience. For further assistance, please contact our customer support.
