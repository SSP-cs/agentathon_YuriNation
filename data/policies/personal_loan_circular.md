# Circular NB/2026: Amendments to Personal Loan

Effective 1 September 2026, Nova Bank is implementing the following amendments to the **Nova Express Personal Loan** guidelines. These changes supersede the previously established terms:

1. **Foreclosure Charges (Total Prepayment)**: Previously, part-prepayments made prior to 24 months incurred a 2.00% fee on the prepaid amount; post 24 months part-prepayments carried zero fee. **Now**, part-prepayments made prior to 18 months will incur a 1.50% fee on the prepaid amount. Post 18 months, part-prepayments will carry a zero fee. This amendment aims to provide greater flexibility and benefits to borrowers who wish to reduce their debt obligation sooner.

2. **Processing Fees**: Previously, the processing fee was a standard non-refundable charge of 1.50% to 2.50% of the gross sanctioned principal, subject to a minimum floor of INR 2,000 and a maximum cap of INR 10,000 plus 18% GST. **Now**, the processing fee is revised to a range of 1.25% to 2.00% of the gross sanctioned principal, with a new minimum floor of INR 1,500 and a maximum cap of INR 9,000 plus 18% GST. This adjustment is designed to lower the upfront costs of securing a loan and improve affordability for our customers.

These updated terms have been instituted to enhance the financial accessibility and flexibility of our personal loan offerings, while continuing to cater to the diverse needs of our valued clients.
